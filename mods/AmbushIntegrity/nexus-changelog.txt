Added Committed Ambush, which briefly preserves sneak eligibility against the exact target acquired through a valid native melee backstab opportunity.
Expanded native backstab reach and added a short same-target availability grace window.
Added conservative Clean Executions that silence the victim's immediate hostile-action broadcast only when no nearby friendly witness is found.
Added optional Backstab Ready crosshair support and transition-based Searching, Detected, and Hidden Again floating-text feedback.
Added Footstep Awareness: ordinary walking and running retain native modifiers while Light or unarmored, Medium, and Heavy or Overload footsteps scale to 1.2x, 1.6x, and 2.0x strength. Crouched movement and native AI behavior remain unchanged.
Made Steel and Bone integration deterministic: its hearing-range scaling composes with Footstep Awareness, and its final damage and material multipliers follow the preserved sneak bonus regardless of load order.
Expanded opt-in decision diagnostics and added cleanly ordered configuration with automatic durable-setting recovery.

Battlecry audio
================

Place up to 10 PCM WAV files in each gender folder. Files are loaded in
alphabetical order, and only the first 10 WAV files in each folder are used.

Recommended names:

male/battlecry_01.wav through male/battlecry_10.wav
female/battlecry_01.wav through female/battlecry_10.wav

The included .wav.placeholder files reserve those names but are not loaded.
Replace a placeholder by removing the .placeholder suffix and supplying a
real WAV file. Keep each battlecry short and free of music or other voices.

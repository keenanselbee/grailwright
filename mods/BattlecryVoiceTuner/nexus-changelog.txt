Held supported native events briefly while their hybrid pitch DSP attaches, preventing the full natural-rate slowdown from reaching the audible onset. Attachment failures now resume quickly with the safe full natural shift.
Added enabled-by-default attribute voice progression with Warrior, Rogue, Mage, Warden, Artisan, Adventurer, Custom, and Disabled archetypes. Warrior defaults to 75% permanent Strength and 25% permanent Endurance, while every curve starts at the Hero's innate values and reaches its configured full depth at attribute value 40.
Added a smooth late-weighted progression curve that keeps early growth subtle and reaches a configurable -6 semitone maximum only for exceptional archetype development. Temporary attribute modifiers remain excluded by default, and Overall Pitch, gender offsets, and random variation continue to layer normally.
Added Natural, Balanced, and Tempo Preserving pitch processing. Balanced is the fresh default and splits the final shift evenly between playback rate and FMOD's pitch-shift DSP, so a -6 semitone voice remains -6 while running about 19% longer instead of 41% longer.
Applied the same bounded hybrid processing to native supported events, custom battlecries, command voices, and their generated acoustic reflections, with automatic natural-rate fallback when a DSP cannot be attached.

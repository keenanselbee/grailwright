[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Battlecry Voice Tuner[/size][/color]
[i]A voice shaped for battle. Sneaking is for cowards, let them hear you come.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Battlecry Voice Tuner[/b] combines three complete features for [b]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon[/b]: a configurable tuner for the Hero's native combat voice, a custom battlecry that affects both the soundscape and nearby enemies, and gender-aware spoken commands for supported mods.

Tune pitch, randomized variation, volume, and supported vocal categories, or let permanent attributes, Soul Vigor, and Blood Essence transform the voice as the Hero develops. Then hold the normal Take All Items action to roar from separate male or female battlecry pools, challenge hostile NPCs, and hear the cry respond to the actual shape of the surrounding world.

The battlecry does not use a simple indoor/outdoor reverb switch. Its advanced geometry-aware acoustic system measures the environment when each cry plays, then builds a location-specific response from room scale, openness, surface distance, direction, and enclosure. Open fields, forests, nearby walls, roofed structures, corridors, cabins, halls, and caverns can all sound meaningfully different.

Optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/254]Eyes in the Dark[/url] integration lets successful cries feed the Wyrd Threat and atmospheric-response systems during eligible Wyrdnights.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]A Voice That Grows With the Hero[/size][/color][/heading]

Voice progression is enabled by default. Instead of assigning one permanent deep voice at character creation, Battlecry Voice Tuner reads the Hero's chosen archetype attributes whenever a supported sound plays. The voice begins at its recorded pitch, changes subtly through early growth, and gains most of its weight as the build becomes truly specialized.

[list]
[*][b]Warrior - default:[/b] 75% Strength and 25% Endurance.[/*]
[*][b]Rogue:[/b] 65% Dexterity and 35% Perception.[/*]
[*][b]Mage:[/b] 75% Spirituality and 25% Perception.[/*]
[*][b]Warden:[/b] 60% Endurance and 40% Spirituality.[/*]
[*][b]Artisan:[/b] 70% Practicality and 30% Dexterity.[/*]
[*][b]Adventurer:[/b] An equal average of all six attributes.[/*]
[*][b]Custom:[/b] Choose the primary and secondary attributes and their exact weighting.[/*]
[*][b]Disabled:[/b] Keep only the existing manual Overall Pitch, pool offsets, and random variation.[/*]
[/list]

The curve measures growth above the game's innate attribute values and reaches full depth at attribute value 40. With the default -6 semitone maximum, the broad shape is approximately neutral at the start, -0.4 at one-quarter archetype growth, -1.8 at halfway, -3.7 at three-quarters, and -6 at full specialization. Permanent BaseValue attributes are authoritative by default, so equipment, consumables, and short-lived effects do not make the voice jump around. An optional setting can include temporary modifiers.

[b]Overall Pitch remains fully useful.[/b] It is the manual baseline. Attribute growth is added to it, followed by the relevant male or female battlecry or command offset and the small randomized variation. Respeccing permanent attributes changes the next supported sound without rewriting the config.

[b]Natural processing is the default.[/b] FMOD's ordinary pitch control changes both pitch and playback rate, preserving the full weight and timing character of the shift; a natural -6 semitone result lasts about 41% longer. Balanced applies -3 semitones through playback rate and -3 through a tempo-preserving pitch DSP, so the same final voice lasts only about 19% longer. TempoPreserving favors the DSP and keeps roughly the original duration. If a DSP cannot be attached, the sound automatically falls back to the full natural shift.

The same final pitch and processing rules apply to supported native grunts, custom battlecries, spoken commands, and their generated acoustic reflections. Native grunts wait briefly when the selected mode needs the DSP path, preventing an unprocessed onset before playback.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]A Demonic Voice Forged by Soul and Blood[/size][/color][/heading]

When [b]Soul and Service[/b] or [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] is installed, every supported native vocalization and custom battlecry takes one progression snapshot when it begins. Soul and Service supplies current Necromantic Power from Soul Vigor; Blood Magic Expansion supplies current Blood Power from Blood Essence. Either progression reaches half audio influence at 1,000 resource and full influence at 5,000.

The blend gives 75% weight to the stronger discipline and 25% to their shared convergence. A Hero can therefore sound strongly necromantic or blood-corrupted through one path, but mastering both is required for the complete effect.

[list]
[*][b]Minimal:[/b] Preserves the original subtle treatment with linear progression, -2.5 semitones, 0.10 Blood distortion, a 5,500 Hz low-pass, and a quiet 100 ms Soul echo.[/*]
[*][b]Demonic - default:[/b] Emerges earlier and reaches -3.25 semitones, 0.18 distortion, a 4,200 Hz low-pass, a stronger 80 ms echo, and a lower Soul-driven shadow beneath the direct voice.[/*]
[*][b]Abyssal:[/b] Emerges earliest and reaches -4 semitones, 0.27 distortion, a 3,200 Hz low-pass, an aggressive 60 ms echo, and a much stronger octave-lower shadow.[/*]
[*][b]Custom:[/b] Uses the individual curve, pitch, distortion, filter, echo, and shadow targets under Demonic Progression - Advanced.[/*]
[*][b]Stable playback:[/b] Progression is sampled once per sound, so gaining or spending resources changes the next voice without morphing one already playing or continuously polling either mod.[/*]
[*][b]Clean commands:[/b] Spoken summon commands are structurally excluded from progression pitch, distortion, low-pass, supernatural echo, and shadow voice so orders remain intelligible.[/*]
[*][b]True vocal scope:[/b] Native SFX_Player_Hit feedback keeps ordinary voice tuning but is excluded from the demonic suite because it is not a Hero vocalization.[/*]
[/list]

Maximum Effect Strength and the native/battlecry scope controls apply to every profile. Soul Vigor drives the echo and lower shadow, Blood Essence drives the visceral distortion, and mastering both completes the pitch and darkness transformation. Battlecry reflections inherit pitch, distortion, and darkness, but do not duplicate either supernatural Soul layer on every synthetic reflection.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Advanced Geometry-Aware Battlecry Acoustics[/size][/color][/heading]

Every successful battlecry takes one bounded acoustic snapshot of the Hero's surroundings. The system converts the measured geometry into diffuse reverb plus only the directional reflections appropriate for that location. It does not continuously scan the world, and it does not give every area the same generic echo.

[b]What that means in play:[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Open fields[/b] stay nearly dry instead of sounding like a small room.[/*]
[*][b]Walls, ruins, and cliffs[/b] can return filtered echoes from the measured direction and distance.[/*]
[*][b]Forests and rough terrain[/b] produce darker, more diffuse responses.[/*]
[*][b]Small rooms[/b] use dense early reverb without replaying the entire cry as an obvious echo.[/*]
[*][b]Corridors[/b] emphasize directional length and early/late balance.[/*]
[*][b]Large halls and caverns[/b] gain longer decay and can return up to three distance-timed 3D reflections.[/*]
[*][b]Open-sided roofed structures[/b] remain lighter than fully enclosed interiors.[/*]
[/list]

[b]How the acoustic system works:[/b]

[list]
[*]Outdoor cries sample 24 directions around the Hero, measuring terrain, vegetation, structures, cliffs, and overhead surfaces.[/*]
[*]Interior and roofed cries sample 30 horizontal, elevated, lowered, floor, and ceiling directions.[/*]
[*]Interior measurements classify open roofs, corridors, small or medium rooms, caves, large halls, and large caverns.[/*]
[*]Measured openness, distance, reflectivity, ceiling height, distance spread, and directional shape drive decay, density, filtering, wet level, and early/late balance.[/*]
[*]Reflection timing uses measured round-trip distance. Interior surfaces must be far enough away to justify a natural repeated cry; closer surfaces contribute to reverb instead.[/*]
[*]No more than three filtered 3D reflection taps are scheduled for one cry.[/*]
[*]The fixed response remains available as a safe fallback when geometry is too sparse or sampling fails.[/*]
[/list]

Custom cries still route through the game's [b]SFX mixer category[/b], so the Master and SFX volume sliders and mute state apply normally. Overall voice volume and a separate battlecry multiplier provide additional control; the tuned default battlecry scale is 0.5.

Yes, it is wildly overengineered for such a random feature, but damn if it doesn't sound accurate.

One order produces one voice regardless of summon count. Each gender and order has its own recent-history memory and avoids its recent clips when alternatives remain.

Command acoustics reuse the same bounded environment measurement as battlecries but apply a lighter response: 0.10 outdoors, 0.45 indoors, and no more than one measured reflection. Separate reusable FMOD paths prevent a command from changing an active battlecry's reverb.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Enemy Aggro and Challenge[/size][/color][/heading]

A battlecry is more than a sound effect. For three active gameplay seconds by default, it challenges hostile NPCs within three times each NPC's own maximum hearing range outdoors or four times that range in interiors and roofed spaces. Each eligible enemy reacts once per cry:

[list]
[*][b]Unaware enemies[/b] receive a strong alert directed toward the Hero.[/*]
[*][b]Already-alert enemies[/b] attempt to enter combat with the Hero.[/*]
[*]Walls and the game's existing hearing checks still matter.[/*]
[*]Hostility, story restrictions, unconscious states, and normal combat safeguards remain authoritative.[/*]
[*]Allies, neutral civilians, summons, dead NPCs, and inactive AI are ignored.[/*]
[/list]

Hearing range and challenge duration are independently configurable. The result can be a controlled taunt, a reckless declaration of war, or anything between them.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Custom Battlecry Pools[/size][/color][/heading]

Keep PCM WAV files directly in the installed plugin's flat battlecry folder:

[code]
audio/battlecry/hero_male_battlecry_00.wav
audio/battlecry/hero_female_battlecry_00.wav[/code]

Each gender pattern accepts up to 15 WAV files in alphabetical order. A configurable recent history avoids the previous two successfully played cries for each gender by default.

The package includes male slots 00 through 14 and female slots 00 through 11. [b].wav.placeholder[/b] files reserve female slots 12 through 14; placeholders are not played. Replace a placeholder with a real WAV to fill that slot.

Versions before 1.2.9 used separate male and female subfolders with battlecry_01.wav through battlecry_15.wav. Move and rename custom files into the flat layout above; legacy gender subfolders are no longer scanned.

Summon command recordings stay directly in the flat command folder:

[code]
audio/command/summon_male_attack_0.wav
audio/command/summon_male_hold_0.wav
audio/command/summon_male_follow_0.wav

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]

Battlecry Voice Tuner replaces the former Player Voice Tuner identity. Its plugin GUID and config path intentionally keep it separate from that older mod.

Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults while preserving compatible customized values. FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab can restore compatible custom values from supported backups. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Troubleshooting[/size][/color][/heading]

If holding Take All Items outside a container does not battlecry, confirm that a real WAV exists for the Hero's current gender and that the cooldown has elapsed.

Enable [b]Diagnostics[/b] and inspect [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b] for the selected WAV, SFX routing, FMOD results, outdoor openness, measured distances, interior room classification, ceiling height, reflection timing, input path, challenged NPCs, and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/254]Eyes in the Dark[/url] response.

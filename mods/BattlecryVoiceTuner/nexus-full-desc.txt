[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Battlecry Voice Tuner[/size][/color]
[i]A voice shaped for battle. Sneaking is for cowards, let them hear you come.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Battlecry Voice Tuner[/b] gives the Hero a voice that grows with them. Tune native combat vocals, unleash gender-aware battlecries that challenge nearby enemies, and hear spoken commands when directing servants through Soul and Service.

Permanent attributes can gradually deepen the Hero's voice, while optional Soul Vigor and Blood Essence progression adds spectral echo, distortion, darkness, and a lower supernatural shadow. Natural, Balanced, and Tempo Preserving processing let you choose how much weight or original timing the transformation retains.

Battlecries use geometry-aware acoustics rather than a universal echo. Open fields remain dry, forests and ruins return restrained reflections, and enclosed rooms, corridors, halls, and caverns develop responses shaped by their surroundings.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Quick Start Guide[/size][/color][/heading]

[b][color=#ffffff]1. Shape the Hero's voice[/color][/b]

The default setup starts at the recorded pitch, adds subtle variation, and gradually deepens the voice as the Hero's Warrior attributes grow. Choose another archetype, set a manual Overall Pitch, or disable voice growth for fixed tuning.

[b][color=#ffffff]2. Unleash a battlecry[/color][/b]

Hold the game's remappable [b]Take All Items[/b] action for 0.45 seconds outside an open container. You can also assign a separate keyboard or joystick-button hotkey. The default cooldown is 1.5 seconds.

[b][color=#ffffff]3. Embrace optional progression[/color][/b]

With Soul and Service or Blood Magic Expansion installed, Necromantic Power and Blood Power can transform supported vocals and battlecries. Soul and Service can also request spoken voices for successful summon orders.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Voice Tuning and Growth[/size][/color][/heading]

Battlecry Voice Tuner supports attack and exertion grunts, hurt and death grunts, status-pain reactions, player-hit feedback, and optional stamina-depleted breathing. Overall Pitch provides the manual baseline, while random variation, attribute growth, and relevant battlecry or command offsets layer on top.

Voice growth is enabled by default and reads permanent attributes whenever a supported sound begins. It starts from the game's innate attribute values, stays restrained through early development, and reaches its configured full depth at attribute value 40. Temporary equipment, consumables, and effects are excluded by default, so the voice does not jump around during ordinary play.

[code]
| Archetype           | Growth source                         |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Warrior - default   | 75% Strength, 25% Endurance           |
| Rogue               | 65% Dexterity, 35% Perception         |
| Mage                | 75% Spirituality, 25% Perception      |
| Warden              | 60% Endurance, 40% Spirituality       |
| Artisan             | 70% Practicality, 30% Dexterity       |
| Adventurer          | Equal average of all six attributes   |
| Custom              | Chosen attributes and weighting       |
| Disabled            | Manual pitch and variation only       |
[/code]

The default maximum contribution is -6 semitones at full specialization. Respeccing permanent attributes affects the next supported sound without rewriting the configuration.

[b]Natural[/b] processing is the default and applies the full shift through playback rate for the heaviest result. [b]Balanced[/b] splits the shift between playback rate and tempo-preserving processing. [b]Tempo Preserving[/b] favors the original duration. If advanced processing cannot attach, playback safely falls back to the natural shift.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]A Demonic Voice Forged by Soul and Blood[/size][/color][/heading]

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/290]Soul and Service[/url] or [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] is installed, supported native vocalizations and custom battlecries take one progression snapshot when they begin. Soul Vigor supplies Necromantic Power; Blood Essence supplies Blood Power. Each reaches half audio influence at 1,000 resource and full influence at 5,000.

The stronger discipline provides most of the transformation, while mastering both completes it. Soul Vigor drives supernatural echo and the lower spectral shadow. Blood Essence drives visceral distortion. Their convergence completes the pitch and darkness treatment.

[code]
| Preset            | Character                                             |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Minimal           | Subtle, linear corruption with a quiet Soul echo      |
| Demonic - default | Earlier growth, stronger darkness, echo, and shadow    |
| Abyssal           | Earliest and most aggressive complete transformation  |
| Custom            | Preserves the current nine editable targets           |
[/code]

Minimal, Demonic, and Abyssal are applied presets. Selecting one writes its nine curve, pitch, distortion, low-pass, echo, and shadow targets into the visible FoA Mod Manager settings. Editing any governed value selects Custom without changing the others; selecting Custom directly changes nothing.

Progression is sampled once per sound, so gaining or spending resources changes the next voice without morphing one already playing. Spoken summon commands remain completely outside the demonic path for clarity. Battlecry reflections inherit the direct cry's pitch and darkness without multiplying its supernatural Soul layers.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Battlecries, Acoustics, and Challenge[/size][/color][/heading]

[soundcloud]https://soundcloud.com/keenanselbee/battlecry-voice-tuner-male[/soundcloud]
[soundcloud]https://soundcloud.com/keenanselbee/battlecry-voice-tuner-female[/soundcloud]

Every successful battlecry selects from the Hero's gender-matched pool, applies the current voice tuning, and takes one bounded acoustic snapshot of the surroundings. The result combines diffuse reverb with only the directional reflections appropriate for that location:

[list]
[*][b]Open fields[/b] remain nearly dry.[/*]
[*][b]Forests and rough terrain[/b] produce darker, diffuse responses.[/*]
[*][b]Walls, ruins, and cliffs[/b] can return filtered echoes from their measured direction and distance.[/*]
[*][b]Small rooms[/b] use dense early reverb instead of replaying the whole cry.[/*]
[*][b]Corridors[/b] emphasize directional length and enclosure.[/*]
[*][b]Large halls and caverns[/b] gain longer decay and up to three distance-timed reflections.[/*]
[*][b]Open-sided shelters[/b] remain lighter than fully enclosed interiors.[/*]
[/list]

Custom cries route through the game's SFX mixer, so Master and SFX volume or mute changes still apply. Overall voice volume and a separate battlecry multiplier provide additional control.

A battlecry also challenges nearby hostile NPCs for three gameplay seconds by default. Unaware enemies receive a strong alert toward the Hero; enemies already alert attempt to enter combat. The default reach is three times each enemy's normal hearing range outdoors and four times indoors or under cover. Walls, hostility, story restrictions, unconscious states, and the game's normal combat safeguards remain authoritative. Allies, civilians, summons, dead NPCs, and inactive AI are ignored.

Yes, it is wildly overengineered for such a random feature, but damn if it doesn't sound accurate.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Spoken Summon Commands[/size][/color][/heading]

When Soul and Service successfully issues [b]Attack[/b], [b]Hold[/b], [b]Follow[/b], [b]Recall[/b], [b]Raise All[/b], [b]Guard[/b], [b]Bulwark[/b], or [b]Hunt[/b], Battlecry Voice Tuner can play one gender-matched command word from the pool made for that order. One order produces one voice regardless of summon count.

The package includes 47 loudness-matched command recordings. Each gender and order keeps its own recent-history memory, avoiding the previous two successful clips when alternatives remain. Passive targeting, retaliation, automatic aggression, and failed commands stay silent.

Commands have independent volume, gender pitch offsets, a short anti-stacking cooldown, and a lighter version of the battlecry acoustic response. They do not challenge enemies, generate Wyrd Threat, or receive Soul Vigor and Blood Essence effects. When Soul and Service owns Sprint + Take All Items for a formation, Recall, or Raise All command, the shared hold is yielded cleanly; the separate battlecry hotkey remains available.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Custom Audio[/size][/color][/heading]

Place PCM WAV battlecries directly in [b]audio/battlecry[/b] using these patterns:

[code]
hero_male_battlecry_00.wav through hero_male_battlecry_14.wav
hero_female_battlecry_00.wav through hero_female_battlecry_14.wav[/code]

The package fills all 15 male slots and female slots 00 through 11. Placeholder files reserve female slots 12 through 14 and are not played; replace one with a real WAV to fill it. Versions before 1.2.9 used retired gender subfolders, which are no longer scanned.

Summon command replacements stay directly in [b]audio/command[/b] and follow:

[code]
summon_{gender}_{command}_{index}.wav[/code]

Supported command names are [b]attack[/b], [b]hold[/b], [b]follow[/b], [b]recall[/b], [b]raiseall[/b], [b]guard[/b], [b]bulwark[/b], and [b]hunt[/b]. The included pools demonstrate every valid name and slot.

[b]Install[/b]

Install with a mod manager, or place the included folder under [b]BepInEx/plugins[/b].

[b]Updating[/b]

Battlecry Voice Tuner replaces the former Player Voice Tuner identity and intentionally uses a separate plugin GUID and config path.

Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults while preserving compatible customized values. FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab can restore supported custom values. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Performance and Troubleshooting[/size][/color][/heading]

Voice progression and optional Soul/Blood progression are calculated only when a supported sound begins. Audio resources and acoustic paths are cached and reused. Environment measurement runs once per successful battlecry or spoken command; there is no continuous world or progression scan. Enemy checks run only during the short challenge window.

If holding Take All Items outside a container does not battlecry, confirm that a real WAV exists for the Hero's current gender and that the cooldown has elapsed. If a summon order remains silent, confirm that its matching gender and command pool exists.

Enable [b]Diagnostics[/b] and inspect [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b] for audio selection, FMOD, input, acoustic measurement, enemy challenge, and integration details.

Better Quick Slots reworks Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon's HUD quick-slot behavior into a predictable keyboard-friendly utility bar.

Features:
- Keeps the large HUD quick slot pinned to the autofill food slot.
- Leaves food on the game's existing quick-slot use key.
- Redirects vanilla quick-slot cycling back to food.
- Repurposes the two small HUD preview positions as Better Quick Slots health and mana potion overlays.
- Moves and scales the vanilla arrow counter into the Better Quick Slots cluster.
- Adds configurable health and mana potion hotkeys.
- Adds configurable quick-slot HUD position offsets and scale, plus arrow-only position and scale tuning.
- Supports Biggest and SmallestSufficient smart selection modes for food, health potions, and mana potions.

Default hotkeys:
- Food: your existing vanilla quick-slot use key.
- Health potion: C.
- Mana potion: V.

Configuration is generated at:
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.better-quick-slots.cfg

Compatibility:
Better Quick Slots patches vanilla quick-slot HUD, hero HUD arrow counter, and HeroItems selection behavior. Mods that replace or reposition the same HUD components, or deeply rewrite quick-slot cycling, may conflict.

TargetVersion=3.2.6
BaselineVersion=3.2.4

Reworked presets into visible, editable ritual-economy profiles. Blood Rite, Desecration, and Exsanguination now write only corpse XP, ritual time, live-drain timing, live-drain XP, and its per-target cap; editing any governed value selects Custom, and the manager refreshes immediately.

Rebalanced every preset around the same 4% live-drain XP per second. Deeper rites now permit longer feeding and greater total rewards rather than granting faster XP, while Exsanguination's maximum combined bonus falls from 100% to 85% of the victim's kill XP.

Made Blood Essence and Blood Power the sole source of Blood/Life and Abhartach strength. The established balanced progression is preserved across every preset, so choosing a faster or richer ritual style no longer weakens or strengthens the spells themselves.

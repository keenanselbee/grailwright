[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Blood Magic Expansion[/size][/color]
[i]Corpse rituals, live draining, and restrained blood-magic progression.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Blood Magic Expansion[/b] turns [b]Blood Transfusion[/b], [b]Life Transfusion[/b], and [b]Abhartach's Calling[/b] into a focused blood-magic progression loop.

This started in the prologue, where I found myself using Blood Transfusion on corpses and pretending I was siphoning them for forbidden power, as one does. Eventually I made the mistake of thinking, "that should probably be a real feature." Several layers of pain later, it has grown into a fairly complete blood-magic expansion rather than a simple spell tweak.

Drain valid corpses for XP and healing, feed on living enemies for small capped XP ticks, and grow stronger through [b]Blood Rite[/b], [b]Desecration[/b], [b]Soul Feast[/b], [b]Spirituality[/b], and corpse quality.

This is not a broad magic overhaul. It is a blood-magic expansion built around corpse rituals, live draining, preset identity, Abhartach corpse effects, and restrained scaling.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]The Loop[/size][/color][/heading]
[code=plaintext]
Kill a blood-plausible enemy
Drain the corpse with Blood/Life Transfusion
Gain corpse XP and immediate healing
Higher-quality corpses improve healing and Abhartach effects
Preset + Spirituality improve Blood/Life spell behavior
Feed on living enemies for capped XP ticks during combat
[/code]

[list]
[*]Corpse XP comes from the enemy's normal kill XP.[/*]
[*]Corpse healing starts from the preset reward, then scales by enemy max HP and corpse quality.[/*]
[*]Successful corpse drains can play a short quality-matched FMOD ritual sound.[/*]
[*]Raised blood spells can cast a configurable red no-shadow inner player light.[/*]
[*]Live drain gives small capped XP ticks while Blood/Life Transfusion damages living enemies.[/*]
[*]Corpse quality affects healing and Abhartach effects. It does [b]not[/b] multiply corpse XP again.[/*]
[*]Bloodless enemies can be blocked by name/type terms so stone, spirits, skeletons, Wyrd variants, and similar targets do not feed the loop.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
Preset is the main user-facing control. It affects corpse ritual timing, corpse XP and healing baseline, live-drain XP rhythm, Blood/Life spell tuning, and Abhartach tuning.

[code=plaintext]
Preset      | Role      | Ritual | XP  | Heal
Blood Rite  | restraint | 1.0s   | 30% | 15%
Desecration | balanced  | 1.5s   | 40% | 20%
Soul Feast  | feast     | 2.0s   | 50% | 25%
Custom      | tuned     | config | cfg | cfg
[/code]

Single-held casting pays half of the effective corpse XP and healing by default. Dual-held Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion pays the full amount.

[code=plaintext]
Preset      | XP tick    | Cap | Tap   | Held
Blood Rite  | 4% / 1.0s  | 20% | 1.03x | 1.00x
Desecration | 8% / 1.5s  | 35% | 1.06x | 1.01x
Soul Feast  | 12% / 2.0s | 50% | 1.12x | 1.02x
[/code]

Live drain timing is reward pacing, not attack speed. Spirituality supplies most late-game spell growth, while held channel speed stays deliberately modest.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[b]BepInEx\config\ks.tgfoa.blood-magic-expansion.cfg[/b]

Version 2.0.0 and newer use a clean GUID and config path. There is no old config migration. The old [b]ks.tgfoa.blood-mage.cfg[/b] and interim [b]ks.tgfoa.blood-mage-expansion.cfg[/b] files are ignored.

Version 2.0.6 enforces [b]ConfigSchemaVersion = 6[/b] because the blood spell inner light adds new visual settings. If the schema marker is missing or outdated, the old config is backed up beside the active file and fresh defaults are generated.

Corpse leech audio is enabled by default. Completed corpse drains choose from low, medium, high, or max FMOD WAV pools based on corpse quality, avoid the last two sounds used in the same tier, and apply a subtle per-play pitch variation. Most users should only touch [b]PlayCorpseLeechSound[/b] or [b]CorpseLeechSoundVolume[/b].

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood Spell Inner Light[/size][/color][/heading]
Raising Blood Transfusion, Life Transfusion, or Abhartach's Calling can cast a red no-shadow point light from the player camera. The light is separate from the vanilla HeroLight, so No Player Light can stay installed while BME provides only the blood-spell glow.

[code=plaintext]
Enabled = true
Intensity = 1.25
Range = 4.0
FadeSeconds = 0.12
[/code]

Lower intensity or range for a subtler effect. Set Enabled to false, or Intensity to zero, for no visual light.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Corpse Quality[/size][/color][/heading]
Each blood-plausible corpse receives a quality value from 0 to 1. When both signals are known, quality is a 50/50 blend of resolved kill XP and enemy max health. If only one signal is available, that signal is used by itself.

[code=plaintext]
ReferenceKillXP         = 300
ReferenceMaxHealth      = 600
MinimumEffectMultiplier = 0.5
MaximumEffectMultiplier = 1.5
[/code]

By default, corpse quality scales Blood/Life corpse healing and Abhartach corpse effects from 0.5x to 1.5x. Character XP is not multiplied again because it already comes from the corpse's kill XP. The 50/50 blend keeps large, lower-XP bodies from outranking similarly valuable monsters from HP alone while still letting enemy bulk matter.

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url] is installed, the blood-magic corpse reticle can scale visually from the same quality. Reticle size is visual-only and defaults to 1.0x to 2.0x Magic reticle size with a low-quality dead zone. Quality changes scale only; valid usable corpses stay the same blood-red color.

[code=plaintext]
Preset      | Damage | Radius | Bleed | Heal  | Search

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Bloodless Filter[/size][/color][/heading]
The default filter blocks targets whose names, templates, or type text suggest they are bloodless:

[b]Stone; Golem; Statue; Construct; Automaton; Crystal; Wisp; Spirit; Ghost; Wraith; Specter; Spectre; Skeleton; Skull; Bone; Animated Armor; Elemental; Wyrdspawn; Wyrdspirit; Wyrd Spirit; WyrdSlime; Wyrd Slime; Wyrdness[/b]

[b]Wight[/b] is not blacklisted by default. Whitelist terms win over blacklist terms.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Performance[/size][/color][/heading]
Corpse rituals use lightweight held-state checks and camera raycasts only while Blood Transfusion, Life Transfusion, or Abhartach corpse feedback is active. The red inner light is one cached no-shadow point light, enabled only while a matching blood spell is raised. Live draining and Abhartach tuning are event-driven from real damage, status, and spell events. Spirituality is cached briefly before spell tuning reads it. Noisy diagnostics default to off; startup and patch-warning logs remain enabled.

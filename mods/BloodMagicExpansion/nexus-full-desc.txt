[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Blood Magic Expansion[/size][/color]
[i]Corpse rituals, live draining, and restrained blood-magic progression.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Blood Magic Expansion[/b] turns [b]Blood Transfusion[/b], [b]Life Transfusion[/b], and [b]Abhartach's Calling[/b] into a focused blood-magic progression loop.

This started in the prologue, where I found myself using Blood Transfusion on corpses and pretending I was siphoning them for forbidden power, as one does. Eventually I made the mistake of thinking, "that should probably be a real feature." Several layers of pain later, it has grown into a fairly complete blood-magic expansion rather than a simple spell tweak.

Drain valid corpses for XP and healing, feed on living enemies for small capped XP ticks, and grow stronger through [b]Blood Rite[/b], [b]Desecration[/b], [b]Soul Feast[/b], [b]Spirituality[/b], and corpse quality.

This is not a broad magic overhaul. It is a blood-magic expansion built around corpse rituals, live draining, preset identity, Abhartach corpse effects, and restrained scaling.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Corpse rituals:[/b] Drain blood-plausible corpses with Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion for character XP and immediate healing.[/*]
[*][b]Restrained progression:[/b] Gain vanilla-scaled corpse XP and small capped live-drain ticks without turning blood magic into an unlimited leveling engine.[/*]
[*][b]Distinct ritual identities:[/b] Blood Rite, Desecration, and Soul Feast shape ritual timing, rewards, live-drain pacing, and spell behavior, while Custom exposes the underlying tuning.[/*]
[*][b]Corpse quality and filtering:[/b] Enemy health and kill XP scale healing and Abhartach effects, while configurable terms reject stone, spirits, skeletons, Wyrd variants, and other bloodless targets.[/*]
[*][b]Blood-magic presentation:[/b] Quality-matched ritual audio, independent red lights that follow each raised blood-spell hand, configurable per-spell and interior intensity, optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/263]First Person Arms Adjuster[/url] alignment, and optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/238]No Player Light[/url] compatibility reinforce the theme.[/*]
[*][b]Optional hitch mitigation:[/b] An isolated, enabled-by-default preload moves the vanilla Bleed graph's cold deserialization into gameplay loading and can be disabled independently.[/*]
[/list]

Each hand light turns off immediately only when that same hand changes from a blood spell to non-blood equipment. Switching the opposite weapon or replacing one supported blood spell with another retains the configured fade behavior.

The shared light intensity defaults to 0.5. Blood Transfusion applies a 0.8x multiplier, Life Transfusion 1.0x, and Abhartach's Calling 1.2x; each value is independently configurable and still combines with interior and temporary casting multipliers.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
Preset is the main user-facing control. It affects corpse ritual timing, corpse XP and healing baseline, live-drain XP rhythm, Blood/Life spell tuning, and Abhartach tuning.

[code]
| Preset      | Role      | Ritual | XP  | Heal |
|-------------|-----------|--------|-----|------|
| Blood Rite  | restraint | 1.0s   | 30% | 15%  |
| Desecration | balanced  | 1.5s   | 40% | 20%  |
| Soul Feast  | feast     | 2.0s   | 50% | 25%  |
| Custom      | tuned     | config | cfg | cfg  |
[/code]

Single-held casting pays half of the effective corpse XP and healing by default. Dual-held Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion pays the full amount.

[code]
| Preset      | XP tick    | Cap | Tap   | Held  |
|-------------|------------|-----|-------|-------|
| Blood Rite  | 4% / 1.0s  | 20% | 1.03x | 1.00x |
| Desecration | 8% / 1.5s  | 35% | 1.06x | 1.01x |
| Soul Feast  | 12% / 2.0s | 50% | 1.12x | 1.02x |
[/code]

Live drain timing is reward pacing, not attack speed. Spirituality supplies most late-game spell growth, while held channel speed stays deliberately modest.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[b]BepInEx\config\ks.tgfoa.blood-magic-expansion.cfg[/b]

[code]
PreloadBleedSkillGraphs = true
[/code]

The Bleed preloader loads its dependency and parent graph on consecutive gameplay-loading frames and retains the parent for the active gameplay domain. It uses no Harmony patches and can be disabled independently from all Blood Magic Expansion gameplay features.

Corpse leech audio is enabled by default. Completed corpse drains choose from low, medium, high, or max FMOD WAV pools based on corpse quality, avoid the last two sounds used in the same tier, and apply a subtle per-play pitch variation. Most users should only touch [b]PlayCorpseLeechSound[/b] or [b]CorpseLeechSoundVolume[/b].

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] is installed, corpse-leech and live-drain XP use red source-specific messages with corpse and magic icons. GFT 1.9.0+ consolidates queued live-drain ticks together and corpse rewards only within the same [b]Meager[/b], [b]Worthy[/b], [b]Potent[/b], or [b]Prime[/b] tier. Blood Magic XP never merges with generic XP or another mod. Older GFT versions keep the styled gains separate. XP still uses the normal character stat path, so multipliers and level-up behavior are unchanged.

Corpse leech sounds use the same quality bands: [b]<=0.25 Meager/low[/b], [b]<=0.50 Worthy/medium[/b], [b]<=0.75 Potent/high[/b], and [b]>0.75 Prime/max[/b].

[code]
ClaimGrailFloatingTextCorpseXP = true
ClaimGrailFloatingTextLiveDrainXP = true
[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Corpse Quality[/size][/color][/heading]
Each blood-plausible corpse receives a quality value from 0 to 1. When both signals are known, quality is a 50/50 blend of base kill XP and enemy max health. If only one signal is available, that signal is used by itself.

[code]
| Setting                 | Default |
|-------------------------|---------|
| ReferenceKillXP         | 300     |
| ReferenceMaxHealth      | 600     |
| MinimumEffectMultiplier | 0.5     |
| MaximumEffectMultiplier | 1.5     |
[/code]

By default, corpse quality scales Blood/Life corpse healing and Abhartach corpse effects from 0.5x to 1.5x. Character XP is not multiplied again because it already comes from vanilla effective kill XP. The 50/50 blend keeps large, lower-XP bodies from outranking similarly valuable monsters from HP alone while still letting enemy bulk matter.

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url] is installed, the blood-magic corpse reticle can scale visually from the same quality. Reticle size is visual-only and defaults to 1.0x to 2.0x Magic reticle size with a low-quality dead zone. Quality changes scale only; valid usable corpses stay the same blood-red color. Corpses restored from save data are reconnected to their owning game location after restoration and remain connected for the current play session, so detection does not depend on loot or collider names. They use the default-size base-color corpse icon and remain non-drainable.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Bloodless Filter[/size][/color][/heading]
The default filter blocks targets whose names, templates, or type text suggest they are bloodless:

[b]Stone; Golem; Statue; Construct; Automaton; Crystal; Wisp; Spirit; Ghost; Wraith; Specter; Spectre; Skeleton; Skull; Bone; Animated Armor; Elemental; Wyrdspawn; Wyrdspirit; Wyrd Spirit; WyrdSlime; Wyrd Slime; Wyrdness[/b]

[b]Wight[/b] is not blacklisted by default. Whitelist terms win over blacklist terms.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Performance[/size][/color][/heading]
Corpse rituals use lightweight held-state checks and camera raycasts only while Blood Transfusion, Life Transfusion, or Abhartach corpse feedback is active. The red hand lights are two cached no-shadow point lights, each enabled only while its hand has a matching blood spell equipped and raised. Their active positions use one cached optional-integration call and at most two transform assignments per frame; reflection is resolved only once. Live draining and Abhartach tuning are event-driven from real damage, status, and spell events. Spirituality is cached briefly before spell tuning reads it. Noisy diagnostics default to off; startup and patch-warning logs remain enabled.

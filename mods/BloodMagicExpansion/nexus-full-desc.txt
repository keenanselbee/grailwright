[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Blood Magic Expansion[/size][/color]
[i]Corpse rituals, live draining, and restrained blood-magic progression.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Blood Magic Expansion[/b] turns [b]Blood Transfusion[/b], [b]Life Transfusion[/b], and [b]Abhartach's Calling[/b] into a focused blood-magic progression system built around draining the dead and feeding on the living.

Drain worthy corpses for healing, XP, and permanent [b]Blood Essence[/b]. Feed on living enemies for smaller, capped XP gains. Then deepen your power through [b]Blood Rite[/b], [b]Desecration[/b], and [b]Soul Feast[/b] presets, and the quality of your victims.

What began as the simple fantasy of siphoning forbidden power from corpses has grown into a complete progression loop of ritual, sacrifice, and restrained scaling, giving blood magic a distinct identity without overhauling the wider magic system.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Corpse rituals:[/b] Drain blood-plausible corpses with Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion for character XP and immediate healing.[/*]
[*][b]Optional Wyrdnight consequence:[/b] With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/254]Eyes in the Dark[/url] installed, successful exposed-Wyrdnight rituals add 4-to-12 Wyrd Threat according to corpse quality.[/*]
[*][b]Blood Essence progression:[/b] Every completed ritual grants uncapped, save-specific Blood Essence from its quality tier. One smooth curve gives a moderate early bonus, reaches normal mastery at Power 100, and continues to Power 120.[/*]
[*][b]Restrained progression:[/b] Gain vanilla-scaled corpse XP and small capped live-drain ticks without turning blood magic into an unlimited leveling engine.[/*]
[*][b]Distinct ritual identities:[/b] Blood Rite, Desecration, and Soul Feast shape ritual timing, rewards, live-drain pacing, and spell behavior, while Custom exposes the underlying tuning.[/*]
[*][b]Corpse quality and filtering:[/b] Enemy health and kill XP scale healing and Abhartach effects, while configurable terms reject stone, spirits, skeletons, Wyrd variants, and other bloodless targets.[/*]
[*][b]Blood-magic presentation:[/b] Quality-matched ritual audio, independent red lights that follow each raised blood-spell hand, configurable per-spell and interior intensity, optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/263]First Person Arms Adjuster[/url] alignment, and optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/238]No Player Light[/url] compatibility reinforce the theme.[/*]
[*][b]Optional hitch mitigation:[/b] An isolated, enabled-by-default preload moves the vanilla Bleed graph's cold deserialization into gameplay loading and can be disabled independently.[/*]
[/list]

Each hand light turns off immediately only when that same hand changes from a blood spell to non-blood equipment. Switching the opposite weapon or replacing one supported blood spell with another retains the configured fade behavior.

The shared light intensity defaults to 0.5. Blood Transfusion applies a 0.8x multiplier, Life Transfusion 1.0x, and Abhartach's Calling 1.2x; each value is independently configurable and still combines with Blood Power, interior, and temporary casting multipliers. Brightness grows from its baseline at Power 0 to 2x at Power 100 and 2.4x at Power 120. Range grows more modestly from 3 meters to 4.5 meters and then 5 meters at the same milestones.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
Preset is the main user-facing control. It affects corpse ritual timing, corpse XP and healing baseline, live-drain XP rhythm, Blood/Life spell tuning, and Abhartach tuning.

[code]
| Preset      | Role      | Ritual | XP  | Heal |
|-------------|-----------|--------|-----|------|
| Blood Rite  | restraint | 1.0s   | 30% | 15%  |
| Desecration | balanced  | 1.5s   | 40% | 20%  |
| Soul Feast  | feast     | 2.0s   | 50% | 25%  |
| Custom      | tuned     | config | cfg | cfg  |
[/code]

Single-held casting pays half of the effective corpse XP and healing by default. Dual-held Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion pays the full amount.

[code]
| Preset      | XP tick    | Cap | Tap   | Held  |
|-------------|------------|-----|-------|-------|
| Blood Rite  | 4% / 1.0s  | 20% | 1.03x | 1.00x |
| Desecration | 8% / 1.5s  | 35% | 1.06x | 1.01x |
| Soul Feast  | 12% / 2.0s | 50% | 1.12x | 1.02x |
[/code]

Live drain timing is reward pacing, not attack speed. Only held-channel damage earns live-drain XP; tap projectiles never qualify, even if they land during another active hold. Confirmed held-channel Blood/Life Transfusion healing is doubled by default through [b]HeldHealingMultiplier[/b] without changing tap projectile healing. Blood Power unlocks late-game spell growth, while held channel speed stays deliberately modest.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood Essence[/size][/color][/heading]
Every successfully completed corpse ritual awards a fixed amount from its quality tier:

[code]
| Tier   | Blood Essence |
|--------|---------------|
| Meager | 1             |
| Worthy | 3             |
| Potent | 5             |
| Prime  | 10            |
[/code]

Blood Essence is never capped. A single continuous curve gives a moderate early advantage over linear growth, grants Blood Power 100 and full intended scaling at 1,000 Essence, then smoothly reaches the hard cap of 120 at 2,000 Essence. That final Power stretch adds another 20% of each fully unlocked gameplay bonus, matching the absolute increase from Power 80 to 100. Successful rituals continue adding and displaying Blood Essence beyond 2,000 even though Blood Power cannot rise further.

At Blood Power 0, preset Blood/Life and Abhartach tuning contributes exactly no bonus. At Power 100, the system reaches its intended full preset and growth-curve balance. Deeds of Avalon is optional and never owns the gameplay progression. [b]GrowthSource = Spirituality[/b] remains available only as an explicit legacy choice.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[b]BepInEx\config\ks.tgfoa.blood-magic-expansion.cfg[/b]

[code]
PreloadBleedSkillGraphs = true
[/code]

The Bleed preloader loads its dependency and parent graph on consecutive gameplay-loading frames and retains the parent for the active gameplay domain. It uses no Harmony patches and can be disabled independently from all Blood Magic Expansion gameplay features.

Corpse leech audio is enabled by default. Completed corpse drains choose from low, medium, high, or max FMOD WAV pools based on corpse quality, avoid the last two sounds used in the same tier, and apply a subtle per-play pitch variation. Most users should only touch [b]PlayCorpseLeechSound[/b] or [b]CorpseLeechSoundVolume[/b].

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] 2.1.2+ is installed, each completed corpse message safely shows both committed rewards on one line, such as [b]+40 XP | +5 Blood Essence[/b]. GFT 2.4.4+ also gives that line the matching Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime corpse icon. If XP cannot be awarded, the pending line is canceled and saved progression is restored. Corpse rewards are never consolidated, so every line reports one ritual's exact XP and Essence. Older GFT versions safely fall back to generic XP text for corpse rituals. GFT 1.9.0+ still consolidates frequent live-drain XP ticks. GFT 2.4.6+ presents visible corpse-ritual, Blood/Life Transfusion, and Abhartach healing through its configurable Red group with the Blood Magic icon, in batches that never merge into ordinary green healing. Correlated held-channel healing ticks remain quiet by default without hiding potions or unrelated immediate healing. Blood Magic XP never merges with another source. XP still uses the normal character XP stat path, so multipliers and level-up behavior are unchanged. Optional diagnostic text defaults on but remains inactive until the matching corpse-rejection, corpse-quality, or inner-light log option is enabled, and shows only low-priority state-change summaries in one collapsed lane.

When [b]Deeds of Avalon 1.6.7+[/b] and Grail Floating Text are installed, Deeds places [b]Blood Essence: X (Y)[/b] immediately above [b]Corpses Drained[/b] in the quick-wheel panel, including at zero; X is integer Blood Essence and Y is Blood Power. Existing Deeds tier counts and their saved quality sum can initialize an older character once when no Blood Magic Expansion progression exists. Blood Magic Expansion then periodically synchronizes its absolute tier ledger so a temporarily unavailable callback cannot permanently drift the display. Failed, interrupted, rolled-back, and incomplete rituals are not reported, and progression works normally without either display mod.

Corpse leech sounds use the same quality bands: [b]<=0.25 Meager/low[/b], [b]<=0.50 Worthy/medium[/b], [b]<=0.75 Potent/high[/b], and [b]>0.75 Prime/max[/b].

[code]
ClaimGrailFloatingTextCorpseXP = true
ClaimGrailFloatingTextLiveDrainXP = true
SuppressGrailFloatingTextLiveDrainHealing = true
HeldHealingMultiplier = 2
ShowGrailFloatingTextDiagnostics = true
OverrideBloodEssence = false
BloodEssenceOverrideValue = 1000
[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Corpse Quality[/size][/color][/heading]
Each blood-plausible corpse receives a quality value from 0 to 1. Native enemy Tier tags set intrinsic quality for nearly the entire roster. Untagged enemies use a fixed shared fallback based on base kill XP and maximum health, keeping Blood Magic Expansion and Steel and Bone in agreement.

[code]
| Native enemy tier | Quality |
|-------------------|---------|
| Tier 0            | 0.050   |
| Tier 1            | 0.125   |
| Tier 2            | 0.230   |
| Tier 3            | 0.425   |
| Tier 4            | 0.625   |
| Tier 5            | 0.800   |
| Tier 6            | 0.900   |
| Tier 7            | 1.000   |
[/code]

Elites gain 0.10 quality, MiniBosses gain 0.175, and Bosses begin at a minimum of 0.875 Prime quality. Relative enemy level then moves quality by 0.025 per level above or below the hero, capped at 0.075 in either direction. This keeps level relevant without letting it overwhelm native enemy identity. The untagged fallback uses fixed references of 700 base XP and 3400 maximum health. Difficulty XP multipliers are not included.

By default, corpse quality scales Blood/Life corpse healing and Abhartach corpse effects from 0.5x to 1.5x. Character XP is not multiplied again because it already comes from vanilla effective kill XP.

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url] 3.0.3 or newer is installed, the blood-magic corpse reticle selects a Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime sprite and can scale visually from the same quality. Registered bloodless, spent, and otherwise blocked corpses retain their tier shape but use a desaturated unavailable color. Reticle presentation is visual-only and defaults to 1.0x to 2.0x Magic reticle size with a low-quality dead zone. Corpses restored from save data are reconnected to their owning game location after restoration and remain connected for the current play session, so detection does not depend on loot or collider names. They retain their tier shape and remain non-drainable.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Performance[/size][/color][/heading]
Corpse rituals use lightweight held-state checks and camera raycasts only while Blood Transfusion, Life Transfusion, or Abhartach corpse feedback is active. The red hand lights are two cached no-shadow point lights, each enabled only while its hand has a matching blood spell equipped and raised. Their active positions use one cached optional-integration call and at most two transform assignments per frame; reflection is resolved only once. Live draining and Abhartach tuning are event-driven from real damage, status, and spell events. Save-bounded Blood Essence syncs to the optional Deeds integration at most once per second. Noisy diagnostics default to off; startup and patch-warning logs remain enabled.

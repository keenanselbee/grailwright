[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Blood Magic Expansion[/size][/color]
[i]Corpse rituals, live draining, and restrained blood-magic progression.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Blood Magic Expansion[/b] turns [b]Blood Transfusion[/b], [b]Life Transfusion[/b], and [b]Abhartach's Calling[/b] into a focused blood-magic progression system built around draining the dead and feeding on the living.

Drain worthy corpses for healing, XP, and permanent [b]Blood Essence[/b]. Feed on living enemies for smaller, capped XP gains. Let Blood Essence deepen your power, then choose [b]Blood Rite[/b], [b]Desecration[/b], or [b]Exsanguination[/b] to shape the pace and reward of your rites.

What began as the simple fantasy of siphoning forbidden power from corpses has grown into a complete progression loop of ritual, sacrifice, and restrained scaling, giving blood magic a distinct identity without overhauling the wider magic system.

[b]For the full experience, these companion mods are highly recommended:[/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/277]Deeds of Avalon[/url], and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url]. Together they provide quality-matched corpse reticles, track Blood Essence and Blood Power on the quick wheel, and surface exact ritual rewards, healing, progression, and diagnostic feedback in the world.

[b]Want to test the complete blood-mage progression?[/b]

In FoA Mod Manager, enable [b]Diagnostics > Override Blood Essence[/b] and set [b]Blood Essence Override Value[/b] to [b]5,000[/b]. This temporarily grants Blood Power 200 and exposes the full mastery and overmastery scaling without replacing your saved Blood Essence. Disable the override when you are done to return to normal progression.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Quick Start Guide[/size][/color][/heading]

[b][color=#ffffff]1. Drain the dead[/color][/b]

Equip Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion, defeat a blood-plausible enemy, and hold the spell over its corpse until the ritual completes. By default, one raised casting hand pays half of the effective corpse XP and healing; two pay the full amount.

[b][color=#ffffff]2. Gather Blood Essence[/color][/b]

Every completed ritual grants permanent, save-specific Blood Essence according to corpse quality. Blood Essence automatically becomes Blood Power and develops all three supported blood spells.

[b][color=#ffffff]3. Feed on the living[/color][/b]

Held-channel Blood/Life damage against living enemies grants smaller XP ticks up to a per-target cap. Tap projectiles remain combat tools and never count as live feeding.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood and Life Transfusion[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Blood Transfusion and Life Transfusion[/b] become two expressions of the same predatory art. Focus either spell on a valid corpse to perform a ritual for character XP, immediate healing, and Blood Essence. The original body and loot remain intact, but that corpse cannot provide Blood Essence twice.

Against living enemies, confirmed held-channel damage grants smaller XP ticks up to a per-target cap. Tap projectiles remain combat tools and never count as live feeding, even when they land during another active hold. Confirmed held-channel healing is doubled by default without changing tap healing.

Blood Power improves tap damage, projectile travel, targeting, Bleed buildup and duration, and cast speed. Held-channel growth remains deliberately modest so feeding does not accelerate out of control. Blood/Life remains direct-hit magic and gains no invented impact area. Completed player-sourced Bleeds retain native duration at Power 0, last 1.5x as long at Power 100, and 2x as long at Power 200; ordinary weapon and enemy Bleeds remain unchanged.

Completed corpse rituals also choose from quality-matched sounds and blood-burst effects. Ritual audio follows corpse distance through the game's SFX mix; set [b]CorpseLeechSoundRangeVolume[/b] to 0 only if you want to disable that distance fade.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Abhartach's Calling[/size][/color][/heading]

Abhartach's Calling turns the corpse itself into the weapon. Corpse quality and Blood Power improve its explosion damage, radius, Bleed buildup, held healing, and corpse search while preserving its native cast behavior. Ritual presets change the pace and reward of feeding, never the strength earned here.

At Blood Power 100, Abhartach gains its full intended progression: +40% explosion damage, +35% radius, +40% Bleed buildup, +40% held healing, and +35% corpse search. Power 200 doubles each fully unlocked bonus portion. Its completed Bleed procs follow the same duration progression as Blood/Life.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood Essence and Power[/size][/color][/heading]
Every successfully completed corpse ritual awards a tier-specific amount:

[code]
| Tier   | Blood Essence |
|--------|---------------|
| Meager | 1-2           |
| Worthy | 3-4           |
| Potent | 5-7           |
| Prime  | 10-13         |
[/code]

Each tier has a guaranteed base return plus a chance to extract additional Essence. Each base point has a 5% bonus chance at Power 0, rising to 10% at Power 100 and 15% at Power 200, subject to the tier maximum above.

Blood Essence is never capped. The established mastery curve gives a moderate early advantage, grants Blood Power 100 and full intended scaling at 1,000 Essence, then transitions to linear overmastery where every additional 40 Essence grants one Power until the hard cap of 200 at 5,000 Essence. Power 200 doubles each fully unlocked gameplay bonus portion. Successful rituals continue adding and displaying Blood Essence beyond 5,000 even though Blood Power cannot rise further.

At Blood Power 0, BME contributes exactly no spell-strength bonus. At Power 100, the system reaches its intended full mastery and growth-curve balance. Blood Essence is the sole spell-growth progression source, with Blood Power retained as its derived gameplay rating; ritual presets never change either one. Deeds of Avalon is optional and never owns the gameplay progression.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Ritual Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
Preset is the main user-facing ritual-economy control. It affects corpse ritual timing, corpse XP and its derived healing baseline, and live-drain XP rhythm. Blood Essence progression governs Blood/Life and Abhartach strength independently.

Selecting Blood Rite, Desecration, or Exsanguination writes five visible values grouped directly below it in FoA Mod Manager: corpse XP, ritual time, live-drain tick time, live-drain XP per tick, and the per-target cap. The values refresh immediately and remain editable: changing any one selects Custom without altering the others. Selecting Custom by itself changes nothing.

[code]
| Preset         | Role        | Ritual | XP  | Heal  |
|----------------|-------------|--------|-----|-------|
| Blood Rite     | restraint   | 1.0s   | 30% | 15%   |
| Desecration    | balanced    | 1.5s   | 40% | 20%   |
| Exsanguination | high payout | 2.0s   | 45% | 22.5% |
| Custom         | current     | config | cfg | cfg   |
[/code]

Single-held casting pays half of the effective corpse XP and healing by default. Dual-held Blood Transfusion or Life Transfusion pays the full amount.

[code]
| Preset         | XP tick    | Cap |
|----------------|------------|-----|
| Blood Rite     | 4% / 1.0s  | 20% |
| Desecration    | 6% / 1.5s  | 30% |
| Exsanguination | 8% / 2.0s  | 40% |
[/code]

All three presets average the same 4% live-drain XP per second. Deeper rites permit longer feeding and greater total rewards rather than generating XP faster.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Corpse Quality and Valid Targets[/size][/color][/heading]
Each blood-plausible corpse receives a quality value from 0 to 1. Native enemy Tier tags set intrinsic quality for nearly the entire roster. Untagged enemies use a fixed shared fallback based on base kill XP and maximum health, keeping Blood Magic Expansion and Steel and Bone in agreement.

[code]
| Native enemy tier | Quality |
|-------------------|---------|
| Tier 0            | 0.050   |
| Tier 1            | 0.125   |
| Tier 2            | 0.230   |
| Tier 3            | 0.425   |
| Tier 4            | 0.625   |
| Tier 5            | 0.800   |
| Tier 6            | 0.900   |
| Tier 7            | 1.000   |
[/code]

Elites gain 0.10 quality, MiniBosses gain 0.175, and Bosses begin at a minimum of 0.875 Prime quality. Relative enemy level then moves quality by 0.025 per level above or below the hero, capped at 0.075 in either direction. This keeps level relevant without letting it overwhelm native enemy identity. The untagged fallback uses fixed references of 700 base XP and 3400 maximum health. Difficulty XP multipliers are not included.

By default, corpse quality scales Blood/Life corpse healing plus Abhartach explosion damage and Bleed buildup from 0.5x to 1.5x. Abhartach explosion radius uses a narrower 0.85x-to-1.15x band, while held healing uses 0.75x-to-1.25x. Character XP is not multiplied again because it already comes from vanilla effective kill XP.

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url] 3.0.3 or newer is installed, the blood-magic corpse reticle selects a Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime sprite and can scale visually from the same quality. Registered bloodless, spent, and otherwise blocked corpses retain their tier shape but use a desaturated unavailable color. Reticle presentation is visual-only and defaults to 1.0x to 2.0x Magic reticle size with a low-quality dead zone. Corpses restored from save data are reconnected to their owning game location after restoration and remain connected for the current play session, so detection does not depend on loot or collider names. They retain their tier shape and remain non-drainable.

Blood magic still requires blood. The default filter rejects targets whose names, templates, or type text indicate stone, bone, spirit, or another bloodless body:

[b]Stone; Golem; Statue; Construct; Automaton; Crystal; Wisp; Spirit; Ghost; Wraith; Specter; Spectre; Skeleton; Skull; Bone; Animated Armor; Elemental; Wyrdspawn; Wyrdspirit; Wyrd Spirit; WyrdSlime; Wyrd Slime; Wyrdness[/b]

[b]Wight[/b] is not blacklisted by default. Whitelist terms win over blacklist terms.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Performance[/size][/color][/heading]
Corpse rituals use lightweight held-state checks and camera raycasts only while Blood Transfusion, Life Transfusion, or Abhartach corpse feedback is active. Direct body hits remain authoritative, while a conservative 0.4-meter surface assist catches near misses without selecting through floors or walls. The red hand lights are two cached no-shadow point lights, each enabled only while its hand has a matching blood spell equipped and raised. Their active positions use one cached optional-integration call and at most two transform assignments per frame; typed HDRP light data is cached and configured once per hand. Live draining and Abhartach tuning are event-driven from real damage, status, and spell events. Save-bounded Blood Essence syncs to the optional Deeds integration at most once per second.

The optional Bleed preloader loads its dependency and parent graph on consecutive gameplay-loading frames and retains the parent for the active gameplay domain. It uses no Harmony patches and can be disabled independently from every Blood Magic Expansion gameplay feature.

One general Diagnostics switch enables throttled targeting, ritual, reward, healing, corpse-quality, and blood-light evidence plus concise Grail Floating Text summaries. It defaults off; startup errors and warnings remain enabled.

Version 1.0.1
Removed the circular Blood Magic Expansion soft dependency while retaining its one-way Deeds statistics integration.
Made Glorious UI delegation honor its master Enabled switch and limited Points available selection to live scene views.
Made event-listener retries transactional so partial binding failures do not duplicate listeners.
Renamed the displayed bounty rows to Recorded active bounty and Highest recorded bounty so existing unobserved faction bounty is not presented as a complete current total.

Version 1.0.0
Added save-backed, per-character statistics with readable JSON export published only after confirmed successful saves and refreshed from loaded save data.
Added a Grail Floating Text two-column quick-wheel panel with level, current/required XP, deeds, total foes, weapon subtype kills, magic spell-name kills, and damage-type fallbacks.
Added configurable row limits, Other rows, right-edge placement, scale, opacity, tooltip-active opacity, and tooltip fade duration; zero-value category rows stay hidden.
Added an option to hide normal quick-wheel item tooltip text while leaving it enabled by default.
Hidden the Points available widget during the quick wheel, while deferring to Glorious UI when its HideGameplayHudInQuickUseWheel setting owns that behavior.
Added Blood Magic Expansion integration using the wording Corpses Drained with Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime quality tiers.
Started with independent counters and no import, migration, or backward compatibility for Killing Blow Mastery statistics.

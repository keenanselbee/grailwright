[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Deeds of Avalon[/size][/color]
[i]Save-backed character deeds and combat statistics, presented beside the quick wheel, pause menu, and gameplay loading screens.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Deeds of Avalon - Character Statistics[/b] records a character's accomplishments inside the active save and presents them through a responsive two-column panel beside the quick wheel, root ESC system menu, and gameplay loading screens.

The panel uses [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] for its fonts, semantic colors, icons, text effects, and charcoal backplates. Statistics tracking and readable JSON export continue to work when GFT is absent; only the presentation panels require it.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Statistics that belong to the save:[/b] Counters are stored per character in the active save instead of a global config or external report.[/*]
[*][b]Three independently toggleable placements:[/b] See level, XP, resources, available points, deeds, progression, and combat totals beside the quick wheel, on the root pause menu, or during gameplay loading screens. Each placement has its own setting, so you can enable any combination. Loading-screen presentation is opt-in; enabling it also activates per-save-slot snapshots so known characters can appear from the beginning of a main-menu load.[/*]
[*][b]Detailed foe attribution:[/b] Track individual one-handed and two-handed weapon types, bows, shields, unarmed attacks, throwables, named spells, damage types, Blood Magic, summons, and fallbacks.[/*]
[*][b]Useful activity records:[/b] Follow exploration, crafting, consumption, fishing, thievery, crime, bounty, rest, deaths, and total Gold earned. Statistics at zero stay hidden, except Deaths.[/*]
[*][b]Highest kills first:[/b] Foe rows sort from highest to lowest by default. Disable the option to restore the authored weapon and magic grouping.[/*]
[*][b]Readable in bright and dark scenes:[/b] Two subtly different charcoal backplates, native SDF outlines, and a soft text backing remain configurable.[/*]
[*][b]Hardened against drift:[/b] Important totals reconcile from their component values, crime reports are deduplicated, failed saves do not publish statistics, and unavailable services retry safely.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Quick-Wheel, Pause-Menu, and Loading-Screen Panel[/size][/color][/heading]
[code]
| Area  | Contents                         |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| Left  | Character, resources and deeds   |
| Right | Total and foe categories         |[/code]

The character summary is one continuous block: Level/XP, HP/MP/SP, Ethereal Cobweb, current Gold, Encumbrance, available Attribute/Skill/Catalyst/Arthur values, and contextual Wyrd Whispers. Zero or exhausted point categories are omitted. A slight gap separates that live summary from the first recorded deed.

Deaths remains the final left-column statistic and is visible even at zero. Foe categories normally appear only after they have a positive count.

[b]SortFoesByKillCount[/b] defaults to enabled. It orders the final visible weapon, magic, Summons, and Other rows from highest to lowest displayed count while keeping Total fixed above them. Equal counts use label order. Weapon and magic limits plus collapsed Other values are resolved before sorting. In this mixed sorted list, weapon and magic fallback or collapsed rows are labeled [b]Other Weapons[/b] and [b]Other Magic[/b]. Disable the setting to restore the authored category order and the shorter [b]Other[/b] label, where position and icons distinguish the two sections.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Recorded Statistics[/size][/color][/heading]
Current values and authoritative totals already stored by the game or a supported integration are available immediately. Historical event counters the game does not retain begin when Deeds first tracks that character and cannot be reconstructed retroactively.

[code]
| Group       | Records                              |
|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| Exploration | Wyrdnights, quests, locations        |
| Crafting    | Recipes and item quantities          |
| Consumption | Food, potions and fish               |
| Wealth      | Total Gold earned                    |
| Crime       | Crimes, locks and pickpocketing      |
| Bounty      | Current and highest                  |
| Rest        | Hours rested and deaths              |
| Combat      | Total, weapons, magic, summons       |
| Blood Magic | Essence, Power, corpse tiers         |
| Necromancy  | Soul Vigor, Necromantic Power        |[/code]

Food uses Grail Floating Text's Orange pool and potions use its Blue pool, matching GFT's consumable notifications. Fish uses Cyan for a distinct aquatic presentation.

Event-backed deed counters begin when Deeds starts tracking that character. Crafted and pickpocketed entries count item quantities rather than callback occurrences. One player crime action counts once even if several crime owners report it. Active Bounty is reconciled from the save's current per-faction bounty values, including bounty that existed before Deeds was installed. Highest Bounty is the highest value Deeds has observed.

Total Gold Earned records positive changes to Gold and ignores spending. It begins at zero when the statistic first becomes available rather than treating current Gold as previous earnings. Its icon grows with the saved total:

[code]
| Wealth tier | Total Gold earned |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Very Low    | 1 - 999           |
| Low         | 1,000 - 4,999     |
| Medium      | 5,000 - 14,999    |
| High        | 15,000 - 39,999   |
| Very High   | 40,000 or more    |[/code]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Foes Defeated[/size][/color][/heading]
Hero-killed NPCs are counted once and classified from the fatal damage source. Enemies killed by the player's summons use the game's dedicated summon-kill event and appear under Summons. Summoned targets are excluded from both hero and summon kill totals.

[code]
| Source | Tracks                                |
|--------|---------------------------------------|
| 1H     | Sword, Axe, Blunt, Dagger, Spear      |
| 2H     | Sword, Axe, Blunt, Spear              |
| Other  | Bows, Shield, Unarmed, Throwables     |
| Magic  | Named spells and nine damage types    |
| Extra  | Summons, Other Weapons, Other Magic   |[/code]

Sickles count as One-Handed Axe. Existing two-handed polearm statistics display as Two-Handed Spear. Every recognized one-handed and two-handed category has its own weapon icon.

[b]WeaponStatisticsMode[/b] controls presentation without reducing tracking detail:

[code]
| Mode     | Shows                               |
|----------|-------------------------------------|
| Detailed | Individual weapon types             |
| Grouped  | One-Handed, Two-Handed and Bows     |
| Both     | Shield, Unarmed, Throwables, Other  |[/code]

Unrecognized one-handed, two-handed, ranged, and general weapon sources retain separate save-backed counters but share one displayed fallback row. With kill-count sorting enabled, the mixed list names its two fallbacks Other Weapons and Other Magic. With sorting disabled, both use Other and remain distinguishable by their authored section and icon.

Named spells retain their own counters and use the dominant recorded damage type for both icon and color. Different spell names that normalize to the same save key receive stable disambiguated keys instead of silently merging.

[code]
| Magic type | Grail Floating Text style |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Fire       | Orange                    |
| Cold       | Blue                      |
| Wet        | Cyan                      |
| Electric   | Gold                      |
| Blood      | Red                       |
| Necrotic   | Necrotic (#25B792)        |
| Poison     | Green                     |
| Pure       | Pale                      |
| Wyrdness   | Semantic Wyrd             |
| Summons    | Pink                      |
| Other      | White                     |[/code]

When kill-count sorting is disabled, named spells remain together and the fallback order is Fire, Cold, Wet, Electric, Blood, Necrotic, Poison, Pure, Wyrdness, Summons, then Other. Necrotic light-cast kills from Soul Rend use Necrotic; Soul Claim remains a protected execution, and kills made by the servants themselves remain Summons.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Save Reliability and Export[/size][/color][/heading]
The authoritative counters live in the active save's GameplayMemory. The readable JSON is a convenient export, not the source of truth.

[code]
BepInEx/config/DeedsOfAvalon/Characters/<character-id>/statistics.json[/code]

Deeds follows the game's save boundary:
[list]
[*]A readable snapshot is captured while save memory serializes.[/*]
[*]The JSON is written only after the game confirms that the save succeeded.[/*]
[*]Loading saved data refreshes the JSON from the loaded state.[/*]
[*]Dying, reloading, or quitting without saving discards those unsaved counter changes.[/*]
[/list]

Deleting [b]statistics.json[/b] does not reset the character. The file is rebuilt after a successful save or after loading saved data. Removing the plugin also does not erase its GameplayMemory context from an existing save.

Total foes, corpse-drain totals, and current bounty reconcile from their authoritative components. Negative values are clamped, counters use saturating addition, multi-owner crime reports are deduplicated to one player action, and loaded-save export waits for GameplayMemory when scene services are still transitioning.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Panel Design and Performance[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Responsive placement:[/b] The panel keeps its 1440p proportions on shorter displays, shrinks further only when needed to fit unusually tall row sets, centers vertically, and remains right-anchored at ultrawide resolutions.[/*]
[*][b]Charcoal backplates:[/b] Two separately seeded procedural black alpha masks provide different mottling, fibers, transparent gutters, and softly irregular edges. Their subtractive design keeps contrast without becoming gray rectangles in black interiors.[/*]
[*][b]Native text effects:[/b] Configurable SDF outlines and underlays provide strong contrast without the dozens of copied text objects required by layered UI shadows.[/*]
[*][b]Tooltip-aware fading:[/b] Normal weapon and spell tooltips remain enabled. The statistics panel fades out by default while either tooltip is visible, then returns smoothly.[/*]
[*][b]Direct presentation lifecycle:[/b] The panel appears as the quick wheel or root ESC menu opens. When Show On Loading Screens is enabled, it stays available during gameplay loading screens; main-menu loads use the exact save slot's last successful panel snapshot before live character data exists, then replace it with authoritative values after deserialization. A slot without a snapshot initializes one when its first load restores live data. Snapshot generation, reads, and writes remain inactive while loading-screen presentation is disabled. Nested save, load, settings, and other overlays still hide the root pause-menu surface. Values refresh five times per second only while a supported surface is open.[/*]
[/list]

Semantic White text receives a small fixed outline-strength adjustment for Level/XP, HP/MP/SP, Total, and contextual point rows. This does not affect icons or Pale text.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Optional Integrations[/size][/color][/heading]
Deeds supports [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] 2.6.7+, [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/254]Eyes in the Dark[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/248]Glorious UI[/url], and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/290]Soul and Service[/url] without making them hard requirements.

[code]
| Mod         | Adds                          |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| GFT         | Menu and loading-screen panels |
| Blood Magic | Essence, Power, corpse tiers  |
| Soul/Service| Soul Vigor, Power, harvest tiers  |
| Eyes        | Semantic Wyrd colors          |
| Glorious UI | Points widget ownership       |[/code]

[b]Blood Magic Expansion 2.6.7+:[/b] Deeds displays [b]Blood Essence: X (Y)[/b], where X is integer Blood Essence and Y is Blood Power. Blood Essence remains uncapped; Blood Power reaches normal mastery at 100 and its hard cap at 200.

[b]BloodMagicStatisticsMode[/b] chooses mutually exclusive corpse rows:

[code]
| Mode     | Rows          |
|----------|---------------|
| Simple   | Total         |
| Detailed | Meager-Prime  |[/code]

Detailed rows use their matching Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime corpse icons. The aggregate Blood Essence row uses the blood-magic icon. Simple-mode Corpses Drained selects an icon from saved average corpse quality, with an empty history resolving to Meager.

Existing tier counts and their saved quality sum can initialize Blood Magic progression for an older character. Afterward, BME periodically synchronizes its absolute tier ledger so a temporarily unavailable callback cannot permanently drift the values. Interrupted, rejected, rolled-back, and incomplete rituals do not count.

[b]Soul and Service:[/b] Deeds displays [b]Soul Vigor: X (Y)[/b] in the dark-green Necrotic color with the necromancy icon immediately after the complete Blood Magic section, including before the first corpse is harvested. X is uncapped, save-specific Soul Vigor and Y is Necromantic Power from 0 to 200.

[b]ShowSoulAndServiceStatistics[/b] controls the complete section. [b]SoulAndServiceStatisticsMode[/b] mirrors the Blood Magic choice: Simple adds one Corpses Harvested total, while default Detailed mode shows Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime harvest rows with matching quality icons in the Necrotic color. Soul and Service periodically synchronizes the absolute progression and tier counts through StatisticsApi v7. Retired Souls Bound and binding-count facts are intentionally not imported.

[b]Eyes in the Dark:[/b] Wyrdnights survived and Wyrdness magic follow its live Purple Wyrdness or Native Orange palette through GFT's semantic Wyrd style.

[b]Glorious UI:[/b] When its master Enabled switch and HideGameplayHudInQuickUseWheel are both enabled, Deeds defers ownership of the Points available widget. Otherwise, Deeds hides and restores only the live widget and never changes Glorious UI's config.

[b]Killing Blow Mastery:[/b] Deeds starts independent statistics and does not read, import, translate, or migrate former Killing Blow Mastery counters or TSV reports.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[code]

[b]Common defaults[/b]
[code]
| Setting                  | Default  |
|--------------------------|----------|
| Enabled                  | true     |
| TrackStatistics          | true     |
| ExportOnSuccessfulSave   | true     |
| Show In Quick Wheel      | true     |
| Show In Pause Menu       | true     |
| Show On Loading Screens  | false    |
| HideItemTooltipText      | false    |
| PanelScale               | 1.5      |
| PanelColumnWidth         | 190      |
| ColumnGap                | 30       |
| PanelBackgroundOpacity   | 0.95     |
| WeaponStatisticsMode     | Detailed |
| SortFoesByKillCount      | true     |
| ShowBloodMagicStatistics | true     |
| BloodMagicStatisticsMode | Detailed |
| ShowSoulAndServiceStatistics | true  |

Additional settings control tooltip fading, opacity, padding, offsets, colors, text outline and backing, row limits, collapsed Other rows, and the Points available widget. Most users can leave them at their defaults. FoA Mod Manager organizes them into focused presentation groups.

[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/size][/color]
[i]Reactive PNG reticles for weapons, targets, stealth, interactions, blood magic, necromancy, and contextual hit feedback.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/b] replaces the vanilla crosshair with customizable PNG reticles that react to your equipped weapon, targeted NPCs, current interactions, optional [b]Ambush Integrity[/b] backstab readiness, optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] corpse feedback, optional [b]Soul and Service[/b] necromancy feedback, and optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/241]Steel and Bone[/url] hit feedback.

[line]

This plugin uses the game's managed Mono assemblies. It is [b]not compatible[/b] with the normal public IL2CPP branch (build 23527157) or an IL2CPP BepInEx installation. BepInEx support for both runtimes does not make plugin DLLs interchangeable.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
In Steam, open [b]Properties -> Betas[/b] and select the [b]mono[/b] branch. Install BepInEx 5 Mono, then install this mod.

Install with a mod manager, or place the included folder at:

[b]BepInEx\plugins\DishonoredDynamicCrosshair[/b]

Do not install older reticle DLLs beside [b]DishonoredDynamicCrosshair.dll[/b].

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[b]BepInEx\config\ks.tgfoa.dishonored-dynamic-crosshair.cfg[/b]

[b]Core[/b] has [b]Enabled[/b] and [b]Preset[/b].

[b]Reticles[/b] has [b]ReticleSizePixels[/b], [b]ShowCenterDot[/b], the four configurable context PNG paths, and scale multipliers for Bow, Magic, and BloodMagic. Bow and Magic default to the General PNG at 0.9x and 1.1x. Every context uses the same hot-reloading dot asset without context scale multipliers.

[b]Colors and Opacity[/b] has [b]DefaultColor[/b], [b]HostileColor[/b], [b]NonHostileColor[/b], [b]IdleOpacity[/b], [b]TargetOpacity[/b], and [b]MountedOpacityMultiplier[/b].

[b]Interaction Icons[/b] has [b]Enabled[/b], [b]IconScale[/b], [b]IconOpacity[/b], [b]CrosshairOpacityWhileActive[/b], [b]HideVanillaInteractionKeyPrompts[/b], and [b]VanillaTextVerticalOffset[/b]. Icons default to 0.8 opacity and 1.1x size, while the underlying crosshair defaults to hidden and the interaction text defaults to 120 UI units above its vanilla position.

[b]Blood Magic[/b] has [b]Mode[/b], [b]RequireRelevantBloodSpell[/b], [b]BloodMagicQualityCrosshairsEnabled[/b], [b]UseCorpseQualityScale[/b], [b]MaximumQualityScale[/b], and [b]UsableCorpseColor[/b].

[b]Steel and Bone Hit Markers[/b] has [b]Enabled[/b], [b]KillingBlowOverlaysEnabled[/b], [b]SizeMultiplier[/b], [b]DamageOverTimeSizeMultiplier[/b], [b]KillingBlowSizeMultiplier[/b], [b]DurationMultiplier[/b], and [b]KillingBlowDurationMultiplier[/b]. Direct-hit frames default to 1.15x the general reticle size, damage-over-time frames default to 1.1x, and complete killing-blow compositions default to 1.3x. All frames default to 1x Steel and Bone's final feedback duration. Killing blows last another 1.5x by default before their corpse tier applies 1.00x, 1.33x, 1.67x, or 2.00x from Meager through Prime. Bow, Magic, Blood Magic, and corpse-quality scales do not affect marker size.

[b]Ambush Integrity[/b] has [b]BackstabReadyOverlayEnabled[/b] and [b]BackstabReadyColor[/b]. The overlay defaults to the killing-blow dark red #8C0003FF.

[b]Advanced[/b] has targeting, sizing, texture filtering, custom awareness-eye opacity multiplier and offset, hands-down behavior, and [b]HideVanillaReticles[/b]. [b]SizeMode[/b] defaults to Reference1440p, preserving the authored 2560x1440 presentation while scaling every crosshair layer by screen height at 1080p, 4K, and ultrawide resolutions. ScreenPixels keeps fixed physical pixels, while UIUnits follows the game's HUD canvas and HUD Scale. The awareness multiplier defaults to 1, making the eye and pupil match the active crosshair opacity exactly. Most users can leave these defaults alone.

When [b]Enabled = false[/b], the custom reticle and background polling pause, and the vanilla target-detection range and crouch indicator are restored.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Center Dot and Crouch Awareness[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]ShowCenterDot[/b] defaults to enabled. General, Bow, Magic, and BloodMagic all use [b]dot.png[/b] at the base [b]ReticleSizePixels[/b] size before context multipliers, while still following the selected [b]SizeMode[/b] and reference-height scaling. The ordinary dot inherits the reticle color and opacity but not Bow, Magic, Blood Magic, or corpse-quality scale multipliers. Blood Magic Expansion and Soul and Service quality reticles suppress the ordinary dot so their corpse silhouettes remain clear.

While crouched, the custom awareness eye replaces the ordinary dot. Frame 0 is a closed central line with long awareness lines outside the reticle. Frames 1 through 9 progressively open the eye while retracting those lines toward the center. Frame 10 is fully open with no outer lines. The eye stays small enough to fit within the Bow reticle while the complete indicator remains readable around every context.

Frames 0 and 1 remain dotless. From frame 2 through frame 10, the shared dot becomes the eye's pupil even when [b]ShowCenterDot[/b] is disabled. It keeps the same context-independent size and follows the eye's color, opacity, and vertical offset. Direct nonlethal hit feedback covers the ordinary dot, while the complete stealth eye and pupil remain underneath every hit-marker layer so awareness is never replaced by damage feedback.

The complete awareness indicator inherits the active crosshair's resolved opacity, including targeting, mounting, interaction, and backstab fading. [b]CrouchIndicatorOpacityMultiplier[/b] defaults to 1 for an exact match and can make the indicator proportionally fainter without disconnecting it from those dynamic states. Blood Magic corpse reticles and routine interaction icons hide the eye and pupil while their contextual presentation is active.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Contextual Interaction Icons[/size][/color][/heading]

Dishonored reads the exact action selected by the game's interaction HUD instead of guessing from localized prompt text. Mining, lumbering, fishing, digging, reading, talking, resting in beds or bedrolls, mounting, using campfires or bonfires, and issuing Soul and Service commands have dedicated 512x512 icons. Attack, Hold, Follow, Behavior, and Raise All each use their own command image. Every Soul and Service command preview retains its current icon until the matching confirmation pulse arrives, without flashing the ordinary crosshair. Attack and individual Hold/Follow pulse for 0.675 seconds. Hold All, Follow All, Recall, Raise All, and Behavior pulse for 1.35 seconds. Items, containers, doors, gathering, searching, and unknown interactions use the general hand. A non-empty corpse, chest, or container keeps the hand visible while its quick-loot panel is open; the icon disappears when the container is empty.

Any locked location uses the lock icon even when its lock is key-only, broken, or otherwise cannot be picked. Theft, pickpocketing, and other illegal actions use the hand in killing-blow dark red. Lock state takes priority over the illegal color.

Routine interaction icons render above the normal reticle, center dot, and awareness eye. [b]IconScale[/b] is independent of weapon and corpse-quality scaling and defaults to 1.1x, [b]IconOpacity[/b] defaults to 0.8, and [b]CrosshairOpacityWhileActive[/b] hides the underlying crosshair by default. Hit feedback temporarily suppresses the routine icon and stays clear. Backstab readiness overrides the routine icon and remains topmost.

[b]HideVanillaInteractionKeyPrompts[/b] defaults to enabled. It collapses the vanilla E, F, or controller-button container so its background fits the remaining action text without empty space. Locked and blocked explanations remain visible; hold-progress graphics inside the hidden button container are hidden with it. [b]VanillaTextVerticalOffset[/b] defaults to -120 UI units, placing the prompt above its vanilla just-below-center position while following the game's HUD resolution and accessibility scaling. Disabling suppression or the plugin restores the vanilla container. All interaction PNGs hot reload.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Ambush Integrity Backstab Ready[/size][/color][/heading]

With [b]Ambush Integrity[/b] installed, Dishonored reads its optional API and displays [b]interaction_backstab.png[/b] only while the exact current target passes the final backstab eligibility result. The 512x512 image uses the standard reticle canvas at a fixed 1.0 scale without an activation pulse. It renders above every other reticle, interaction, and hit-marker layer, while all underlying crosshair elements are dimmed to half their normal opacity.

Dishonored does not reproduce crouch, visibility, target-lock, range, or grace rules. Ambush Integrity owns that gameplay truth. Target changes, eligibility loss, or stale state remove the overlay immediately. Committed Ambush does not present itself as a guaranteed backstab action.

The integration is optional. Without Ambush Integrity, all ordinary reticle behavior remains unchanged.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood Magic Corpse Drain Reticle[/size][/color][/heading]

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] installed, Dishonored selects [b]custom_reticle_bloodmagic_0.png[/b] through [b]custom_reticle_bloodmagic_3.png[/b] for Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime. Frame 0 also serves as the fallback when quality is unavailable, tiered reticles are disabled, or a tier asset is missing. Their complete size is ReticleSizePixels x MagicScale x BloodMagicScale x corpse-quality scale, so the default 1.1 MagicScale is inherited before the Blood Magic and quality multipliers. Set [b]Mode = Off[/b] to disable the integration. Enable [b]RequireRelevantBloodSpell[/b] to show it only while Blood/Life Transfusion or Abhartach's Calling is relevant.

[list]
[*][b]No focused corpse:[/b] No blood-magic reticle appears.[/*]
[*][b]Registered blocked, bloodless, or spent corpse:[/b] Retains its Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime PNG at 1x and uses the ordinary [b]DefaultColor[/b] and [b]IdleOpacity[/b].[/*]
[*][b]Unregistered or unresolved corpse:[/b] Uses the original fallback blood-magic PNG with the ordinary [b]DefaultColor[/b] and [b]IdleOpacity[/b].[/*]
[*][b]Usable or channeling corpse:[/b] Uses [b]UsableCorpseColor[/b] and can also scale from 1x up to [b]MaximumQualityScale[/b]. Quality changes sprite and size, not color.[/*]
[*][b]Living enemy:[/b] Keeps the normal hostile or Magic reticle.[/*]
[/list]

If Blood Magic Expansion is not loaded, Dishonored marks the optional API unavailable for the rest of the game session instead of repeatedly searching for it.

[line]

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Soul Rend Quality Reticle[/size][/color][/heading]

With [b]Soul and Service[/b] installed and Soul Rend equipped, Dishonored uses the same Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime frames for eligible corpses and active owned summons. They are tinted #22A886 Necrotic green, carrying the hue of #25B792 while retaining the previous reticle intensity. Corpse quality determines both the frame and the configured quality-size scaling; active raised servants retain their source corpse tier. While heavy Soul Rend is held over a relevant servant, [b]custom_reticle_necromagic_heal.png[/b] appears for Restore Servant and Servant Fully Restored, while [b]custom_reticle_necromagic_empower.png[/b] appears for Empower Servant. Restore and Empower remain saturated Necrotic green, while Requires Soul Vigor and Servant Fully Restored match Blood Magic's desaturated unavailable presentation. Fully restored servants rely on that desaturated icon without interaction text, and feedback-only Soul Rend hovers add no generic interaction hand. Both PNGs hot-reload with the other reticle assets. Dedicated Attack, Hold, Follow, and Behavior icons replace the general hand for command interactions and pulse when their command succeeds. No generic command icon is used.

The integration remains inactive for ordinary targets, ineligible corpses, and summons not owned by the hero. It uses Soul and Service's optional API and does not change resurrection rules or summon statistics.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Steel and Bone Hit Markers[/size][/color][/heading]

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/241?tab=description]Steel and Bone[/url] installed, each outgoing player hit briefly replaces the outer context reticle with a hit marker above the center visuals:

[code]| Hit Marker | Visual effectiveness | Meaning                       |
|------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
|      0     | Immune or zero       | Blocked                       |
|      1     | Below x0.35          | Extreme resistance            |
|      2     | x0.35 to below x0.70 | Strong resistance             |
|      3     | x0.70 to below x0.95 | Mild resistance               |
|      4     | x0.95 to x1.05       | Shared custom_reticle.png     |
|      5     | Above x1.05 to x1.10 | Mild weakness                 |
|      6     | Above x1.10 to x1.20 | Strong weakness               |
|      7     | Above x1.20          | Extreme weakness              |[/code]

Every direct nonlethal hit adds a central diamond over its effectiveness frame, colored with Steel and Bone's final damage-feedback color. The diamond covers the ordinary dot but remains above, rather than replacing, the crouch-awareness eye. Damage-over-time ticks and killing blows omit the diamond. Steel and Bone's presentation-adjusted effectiveness and calculated red shift remain intact for weak spots and critical hits. Its single Effectiveness Feedback Sensitivity value defaults to x1.20 on Tempered, x1.10 on Hardened, and x1.00 on Crucible, and changes presentation without changing actual damage. Weak-spot and critical symbols are independent overlays above the diamond, so either or both can appear over any effectiveness frame. A killing blow adds its corpse-tier overlay above both precision layers: files 0, 1, 2, and 3 correspond to Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime. It turns the base frame plus every visible overlay dark red (#8C0003). Floating damage numbers can remain disabled; the hit markers still work.

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/234]Killing Blow Mastery[/url] 1.9.4 and Steel and Bone 4.0.1, the exact corpse-quality overlay also becomes the Execute indicator. It appears white while Execute is available, keeps that Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime shape throughout the finisher, and follows real normalized animation progress toward #8C0003. The exact target's killing-blow payload waits behind the cinematic. At 90% progress, the white-to-red preview hands off to the normal effectiveness, weak-spot, critical, and corpse-quality composition. Exiting without confirmed target death clears the preview without presenting a kill.

By default, [b]IncludeSummonAttacks[/b] is enabled so both the hero and hero-owned summons produce hit markers. Hero markers replace summon markers immediately, while summon markers cannot interrupt an active hero marker. The latest hit still wins within the same source priority. Disable the setting for hero-only feedback. Direct-hit markers display at 1.15x the General reticle size, damage-over-time tick markers display at 1.1x, and complete killing-blow compositions display at 1.3x. All use 1x Steel and Bone's final feedback duration. Bow, Magic, Blood Magic, and corpse-quality size scales do not affect them. After the quick settle and fade, the current General, Bow, Magic, or Blood Magic reticle returns. Without Steel and Bone, Dishonored keeps its normal crosshair behavior.

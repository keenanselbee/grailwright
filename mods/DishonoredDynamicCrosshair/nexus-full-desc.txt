[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/size][/color]
[i]Reactive PNG reticles for weapons, targets, blood magic, and contextual hit feedback.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/b] replaces the vanilla crosshair with customizable PNG reticles that react to your equipped weapon, targeted NPCs, optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] corpse feedback, and optional [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/241]Steel and Bone[/url] hit feedback.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Reactive reticles:[/b] Use separate hot-reloading PNGs for general, bow, magic, and blood-magic corpse contexts.[/*]
[*][b]Flexible visibility:[/b] Choose AlwaysVisible, TargetOnly, CombatReady, or HostilesOnly, with live hostility updates and configurable target range.[/*]
[*][b]Readable targeting:[/b] Apply shared default, hostile, and friendly or neutral colors while retaining independent crouch-indicator controls.[/*]
[*][b]Precise presentation:[/b] Set true screen-pixel or canvas-relative dimensions, texture filtering, mounted opacity, and vanilla-reticle suppression.[/*]
[*][b]Blood Magic integration:[/b] Show Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime corpse-drain reticles through the optional [b]Blood Magic Expansion[/b] API.[/*]
[*][b]Steel and Bone hit markers:[/b] Temporarily replace the current reticle with eight effectiveness frames, independent weak-spot and critical overlays, and a tiered killing-blow layer above them.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Requirements[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon on the Mono branch.[/*]
[*]BepInEx 5 for the Mono build.[/*]
[*]Supports Windows and Linux.[/*]
[/list]

This plugin uses the game's managed Mono assemblies. It is [b]not compatible[/b] with the normal public IL2CPP branch (build 23527157) or an IL2CPP BepInEx installation. BepInEx support for both runtimes does not make plugin DLLs interchangeable.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
In Steam, open [b]Properties -> Betas[/b] and select the [b]mono[/b] branch. Install BepInEx 5 Mono, then install this mod.

Install with a mod manager, or place the included folder at:

[b]BepInEx\plugins\DishonoredDynamicCrosshair[/b]

Do not install older reticle DLLs beside [b]DishonoredDynamicCrosshair.dll[/b].

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[b]BepInEx\config\ks.tgfoa.dishonored-dynamic-crosshair.cfg[/b]

[b]Core[/b] has [b]Enabled[/b] and [b]Preset[/b].

[b]Reticles[/b] has [b]ReticleSizePixels[/b], the four PNG paths, and scale multipliers for Bow, Magic, and BloodMagic. General uses the global size directly.

[b]Colors and Opacity[/b] has [b]DefaultColor[/b], [b]HostileColor[/b], [b]NonHostileColor[/b], [b]IdleOpacity[/b], [b]TargetOpacity[/b], and [b]MountedOpacityMultiplier[/b].

[b]Blood Magic[/b] has [b]Mode[/b], [b]RequireRelevantBloodSpell[/b], [b]BloodMagicQualityCrosshairsEnabled[/b], [b]UseCorpseQualityScale[/b], [b]MaximumQualityScale[/b], and [b]UsableCorpseColor[/b].

[b]Steel and Bone Hit Markers[/b] has [b]Enabled[/b], [b]KillingBlowOverlaysEnabled[/b], [b]SizeMultiplier[/b], [b]DamageOverTimeSizeMultiplier[/b], [b]KillingBlowSizeMultiplier[/b], [b]DurationMultiplier[/b], and [b]KillingBlowDurationMultiplier[/b]. Direct-hit frames default to 1.15x the general reticle size, damage-over-time frames default to 1.1x, and complete killing-blow compositions default to 1.3x. All frames default to 1x Steel and Bone's final feedback duration. Killing blows last another 1.5x by default before their corpse tier applies 1.00x, 1.33x, 1.67x, or 2.00x from Meager through Prime. Bow, Magic, Blood Magic, and corpse-quality scales do not affect marker size.

[b]Advanced[/b] has targeting, sizing, texture filtering, crouch indicator opacity/offset, hands-down behavior, and [b]HideVanillaReticles[/b]. Most users can leave these defaults alone.

When [b]Enabled = false[/b], the custom reticle and background polling pause, and vanilla target-detection range/crouch opacity are restored.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]AlwaysVisible[/b]: General, Bow, and Magic are always visible and use smart target colors.[/*]
[*][b]TargetOnly[/b]: hides normal reticles unless a hostile, friendly, or neutral NPC is targeted.[/*]
[*][b]CombatReady[/b]: General is target-only, while Bow and Magic remain visible.[/*]
[*][b]HostilesOnly[/b]: all normal reticles appear only over hostile NPCs.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Blood Magic Corpse Drain Reticle[/size][/color][/heading]

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/224]Blood Magic Expansion[/url] installed, Dishonored selects a Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime corpse reticle from the shared corpse-quality bucket. The original [b]custom_reticle_bloodmagic.png[/b] remains the fallback when quality is unavailable, tiered reticles are disabled, or a tier asset is missing. Set [b]Mode = Off[/b] to disable the integration. Enable [b]RequireRelevantBloodSpell[/b] to show it only while Blood/Life Transfusion or Abhartach's Calling is relevant.

[list]
[*][b]No focused corpse:[/b] No blood-magic reticle appears.[/*]
[*][b]Registered blocked, bloodless, or spent corpse:[/b] Retains its Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime PNG at 1x and uses the ordinary [b]DefaultColor[/b] and [b]IdleOpacity[/b].[/*]
[*][b]Unregistered or unresolved corpse:[/b] Uses the original fallback blood-magic PNG with the ordinary [b]DefaultColor[/b] and [b]IdleOpacity[/b].[/*]
[*][b]Usable or channeling corpse:[/b] Uses [b]UsableCorpseColor[/b] and can also scale from 1x up to [b]MaximumQualityScale[/b]. Quality changes sprite and size, not color.[/*]
[*][b]Living enemy:[/b] Keeps the normal hostile or Magic reticle.[/*]
[/list]

If Blood Magic Expansion is not loaded, Dishonored marks the optional API unavailable for the rest of the game session instead of repeatedly searching for it.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Steel and Bone Hit Markers[/size][/color][/heading]

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/241?tab=description]Steel and Bone[/url] installed, each outgoing player hit briefly replaces the current reticle with a hit marker showing how effective that hit was:

[code]| Hit Marker | Visual effectiveness | Meaning                       |
|------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
|      0     | Immune or zero       | Blocked                       |
|      1     | Below x0.35          | Extreme resistance            |
|      2     | x0.35 to below x0.70 | Strong resistance             |
|      3     | x0.70 to below x0.95 | Mild resistance               |
|      4     | x0.95 to x1.05       | Neutral; also General default |
|      5     | Above x1.05 to x1.10 | Mild weakness                 |
|      6     | Above x1.10 to x1.20 | Strong weakness               |
|      7     | Above x1.20          | Extreme weakness              |[/code]

Every direct nonlethal hit adds a central diamond over its effectiveness frame, colored with Steel and Bone's final damage-feedback color. Damage-over-time ticks and killing blows omit the diamond. Steel and Bone's presentation-adjusted effectiveness and calculated red shift remain intact for weak spots and critical hits. Its single Effectiveness Feedback Sensitivity value defaults to x1.20 on Tempered, x1.10 on Hardened, and x1.00 on Crucible, and changes presentation without changing actual damage. Weak-spot and critical symbols are independent overlays above the diamond, so either or both can appear over any effectiveness frame. A killing blow adds its Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime corpse-tier overlay above both precision layers and turns the base frame plus every visible overlay dark red (#8C0003). Floating damage numbers can remain disabled; the hit markers still work.

By default, direct-hit markers display at 1.15x the General reticle size, damage-over-time tick markers display at 1.1x, and complete killing-blow compositions display at 1.3x. All use 1x Steel and Bone's final feedback duration. Bow, Magic, Blood Magic, and corpse-quality size scales do not affect them. A new hit immediately selects the latest frame and restarts the timer. After the quick settle and fade, the current General, Bow, Magic, or Blood Magic reticle returns. Without Steel and Bone, Dishonored keeps its normal crosshair behavior.

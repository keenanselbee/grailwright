[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Enemy Respawn Control[/size][/color]
[i]Control hostile enemy respawn timing with game-world time presets.[/i][/center]

[line]

Enemy Respawn Control is a standalone BepInEx 5 Mono plugin for [b]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon[/b]. It remembers regular hostile enemy spawners after their spawned enemies are killed and blocks future respawn attempts until the configured delay has elapsed.

The default [b]Default[/b] preset waits 24 in-game/weather hours. This is still a large increase over the game's usual 2-hour fallback while keeping the world from feeling permanently empty.

ERC measures respawn delays with the game's world/weather clock, not real time. Timescale and day-night mods can make these waits take more or less real-world time because the delay follows the in-game clock.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]What It Does[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Guards cleared hostile enemy spawners after their spawned enemies are killed.[/*]
[*]Blocks respawn checks and direct spawn attempts until the configured delay has elapsed.[/*]
[*]Ignores resource, pickable, and passive non-enemy location spawners.[/*]
[*]Leaves ambush-only spawners, Wyrd-night spawner state, and manual/story-triggered spawns alone while keeping ordinary spawners controlled.[/*]
[*]Uses in-game/weather time only, so day-night speed changes are reflected in real-world wait time.[/*]
[*]Offers presets from vanilla-style timing to indefinitely blocked cleared spawners.[/*]
[*]Includes diagnostics for spawner lock creation, blocked gate names, allowed spawn attempts, skipped non-enemy spawners, cleanup, and lock expiry decisions.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Presets[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Vanilla[/b] - 2 in-game/weather hours[/*]
[*][b]Fast[/b] - 6 in-game/weather hours[/*]
[*][b]Default[/b] - 24 in-game/weather hours[/*]
[*][b]Slow[/b] - 72 in-game/weather hours[/*]
[*][b]Very Slow[/b] - 168 in-game/weather hours[/*]
[*][b]Custom[/b] - CustomRespawnHours[/*]
[*][b]Disabled[/b] - block cleared hostile enemy spawners indefinitely[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.enemy-respawn-control.cfg[/code]

Generated defaults use the Default preset and diagnostics off. All preset and custom durations are measured in in-game/weather time. There is no real-time mode.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
Install with Vortex as a [b]BepInEx plugin[/b], or place the plugin folder here:

[code]
BepInEx/plugins/EnemyRespawnControl/EnemyRespawnControl.dll[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Notes[/size][/color][/heading]
Disable or remove older RespawnTimer-style mods while testing Enemy Respawn Control. If an area still respawns too quickly, enable [b]Diagnostics[/b] and check [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b] for spawner lock, blocked gate, skipped non-enemy spawner, special-spawn bypass, and cleanup messages.

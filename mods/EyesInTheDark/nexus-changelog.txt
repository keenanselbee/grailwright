Nearby ordinary guards can now join active official hunts against every exact Eyes-spawned hunter, including sidecars, and keep responding if the Hero enters Wyrd protection during the same exterior hunt. Both the guard's distance from the Hero and its distance from the hunter must remain within the configurable response radius. Assistance is optional and capped, excludes unique and special actors, and never changes factions permanently or targets ambient stalkers.

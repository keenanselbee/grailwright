TargetVersion=1.4.0
BaselineVersion=1.3.9
Gameplay presets are now durable and transparent in FoA Mod Manager. Uneasy Night, Watchful Night, and Cursed Night write every governed value shown in three adjacent preset sections; editing any of those values switches the selector to Custom, and choosing Custom directly changes nothing. The manager refreshes after a preset change so the displayed values stay current. Existing customized gameplay tuning is retained conservatively as Custom through the schema update.

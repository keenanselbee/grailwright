[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]First Person Arms Adjuster[/size][/color]
[i]Get weapons away from your face. Adjust their position without changing FOV or aim.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]First Person Arms Adjuster[/b] moves the rendered first-person body, arms, weapons, shields, and held arrows in camera space while leaving the world camera and gameplay transforms alone.

Use it to push an oversized viewmodel farther away, bring it closer, or make small horizontal and vertical corrections without replacing the game's field of view.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*][b]Full position control:[/b] Move your first-person body, arms, and equipment farther away, closer, left, right, up, or down.[/*]
[*][b]Equipment-specific depth:[/b] Set separate forward positions for melee weapons, bows, and magic, with one general value for everything else.[/*]
[*][b]Stable weapons and effects:[/b] Keep multi-part weapons, two-handed equipment, and attached visual effects aligned through movement and animation.[/*]
[*][b]Cleaner heavy attacks:[/b] Use separate held-attack adjustments to keep intrusive shoulder and torso geometry out of view.[/*]
[*][b]Smooth transitions:[/b] Ease toward the vanilla position during two-handed sheathing and bonfire interactions, then smoothly restore your chosen offsets.[/*]
[*][b]Live tuning:[/b] See positioning changes immediately without restarting the game.[/*]
[*][b]Visual changes only:[/b] Leave FOV, aiming, projectile origins, hit detection, physics, and saved player position untouched.[/*]
[*][b]Optional mod integration:[/b] Let compatible effects follow adjusted hands without moving gameplay attachment points.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Positioning[/size][/color][/heading]
Offsets are measured in meters relative to the camera:

[list]
[*]Positive [b]ForwardOffset[/b] moves the first-person model farther from the camera.[/*]
[*]Positive [b]HorizontalOffset[/b] moves it to the right.[/*]
[*]Positive [b]VerticalOffset[/b] moves it upward.[/*]
[/list]

Category offsets are enabled by default. Melee weapons and magic use 0.30, while bows use the smaller 0.10 offset. Start with forward values between 0.20 and 0.35. Set every offset to 0 or disable the plugin to restore the vanilla presentation.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.first-person-arms-adjuster.cfg
[/code]

FoA Mod Manager organizes the live controls into [b]General[/b], [b]Position[/b], [b]Equipment Depth[/b], [b]Advanced - Held Melee[/b], [b]Advanced - Effects[/b], and [b]Diagnostics[/b]. Friendly labels show metres and signed directions, while the stored config keys in the table remain unchanged.

[code]
| Setting                    | Default | Purpose                                      |
|----------------------------|---------|----------------------------------------------|
| Enabled                    | true    | Master switch                                |
| UseCategoryForwardOffsets  | true    | Use separate melee, bow, and magic values    |
| AdjustAttachedEffects      | true    | Align equipped presentation effects          |
| ForwardOffset              | 0.30    | General and unrecognized-equipment depth     |
| HorizontalOffset           | 0.0     | Right or left camera-space adjustment        |
| VerticalOffset             | 0.0     | Up or down camera-space adjustment           |
| MeleeForwardOffset         | 0.30    | Melee weapon depth                           |
| BowForwardOffset           | 0.10    | Bow depth                                    |
| MagicForwardOffset         | 0.30    | Equipped-magic depth                         |
| MitigateHeldMeleeBodyIntrusion | true | Reduce offsets during held melee attacks    |
| HeldMeleeOffsetScale       | 1.0     | Fraction of the normal offset kept while held|

[/code]

All position values accept a range from -0.50 to 0.50 meters.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Rendering Scope And Limitations[/size][/color][/heading]
The game uses one first-person body hierarchy, so visible torso or leg geometry may move with the arms. Held one-handed, two-handed, dual-wielded, and alternate melee heavy attacks are handled specially: the complete offset eases toward the configured held-melee scale while the attack is raised or held, then applies the held-only forward and vertical corrections. The default scale retains the normal offset and pulls the held pose 0.05 metres toward the camera near plane and 0.05 metres down. Everything eases back after release or cancellation. When a melee weapon is sheathed in its active two-handed grip, the complete presentation offset eases to the vanilla position before the game hides the viewmodel, then restores safely after the transition or an interruption. One-handed grips, bows, spells, and unarmed states retain their normal presentation throughout sheathing. Versatile Weapons is supported automatically through its live grip classification. Bonfire interactions similarly ease to zero during the crouch, remain at the vanilla position through bonfire submenus and the complete controller stand-up transition, then ease back in. Test melee weapons, bows, shields, magic, item use, swimming, and mounted gameplay when choosing offsets.

The plugin refreshes the active rig and equipment regions repeatedly, caches only the small active set of equipped presentation-effect roots, clears cached rig and render references during scene changes, and briefly suspends offsets while a replacement scene settles. Effects beneath the first-person body keep an adjusted emitter position between frames, then temporarily remove that child offset while the body-root render translation is active so local-space effects move exactly once. It changes presentation data only; the live animation skeleton and gameplay transforms remain untouched. AdjustAttachedEffects can disable the effect correction independently.

Bows use their normal category offset without a special full-draw hand correction. Released arrows originate from the unchanged gameplay fire point, so a small visual transition can remain when a held arrow becomes a projectile.

Because this mod adjusts native Kandra and Drake rendering data, treat unusually large offsets as experimental and test them across the activities you use.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

Automatically preserved: Enabled, MitigateHeldMeleeBodyIntrusion, ForwardOffset, HorizontalOffset, VerticalOffset, MeleeForwardOffset, BowForwardOffset, MagicForwardOffset, HeldMeleeOffsetScale, HeldMeleeExtraForwardOffset, HeldMeleeExtraVerticalOffset, and Diagnostics.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Troubleshooting[/size][/color][/heading]
If arms or equipment appear clipped, hidden, or too distant, reduce the active category offset first. Set all offsets to 0 or disable [b]Enabled[/b] to confirm the vanilla presentation before testing another value.

Enable [b]Diagnostics[/b] to log the resolved first-person hierarchy, the Kandra rig, culling and linked Drake equipment paths receiving the offset, the cached equipped-effect count, and melee FSM state changes with their held-mitigation result. Full details are written to [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b].

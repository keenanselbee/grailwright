[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Glorious UI[/size][/color]
[i]A centered gameplay HUD, smarter utilities, and a six-loadout Equipment and quick-use suite.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Glorious UI[/b] is a resolution-independent interface suite for [b]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon[/b]. It centers and organizes the gameplay HUD, adds smart consumable controls, expands Equipment with six virtual quick slots and weapon loadouts, rebuilds the quick-use wheel around those loadouts, and quiets intrusive navigation and notification elements.

Each major area has its own ownership switch. Disable an area to return that part of the interface to vanilla behavior without giving up the rest of the suite.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Centered Gameplay HUD[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Anchors the health, mana, and stamina HUD to Bottom Center with the tuned -72 X, -25 Y, and 0.9 scale built into the layout.[/*]
[*]Keeps the composition consistent at 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3440x1440, and other resolutions or aspect ratios.[/*]
[*]Provides live anchor, offset, scale, and visible-duration controls over neutral 0, 0, and 1 adjustment values.[/*]
[*]Keeps the Hero HUD visible for two seconds after the game refreshes it by default.[/*]
[*]Places the Quick Slot HUD at a built-in 196 X, 39.1 Y, and 0.8 scale beneath the centered bars.[/*]
[*]Moves the arrow counter into the utility cluster with a built-in -7 X, -7 Y, and 1.38 scale and larger quantity text.[/*]
[*]Controls the Wyrd Power HUD with a built-in -2 X, -5 Y, and 0.9 scale while hiding its use prompt without disabling the input.[/*]
[/list]

When [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/254]Eyes in the Dark[/url] is installed, Glorious UI requests that its Eyes-owned Wyrd Threat meter sit below the vanilla resource bars. Eyes remains responsible for creating, updating, hiding, and destroying the meter; Glorious UI never creates a duplicate.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Six-Loadout Quick-Use Wheel[/size][/color][/heading]
The quick-use wheel presents Glorious weapon loadouts 1 through 6. Loadouts 1 through 4 retain the vanilla left and upper positions; loadouts 5 and 6 replace the two bottom consumable wedges. Virtual quick slots and the autofill food slot remain available through the gameplay HUD and Equipment panel.

[list]
[*]Routes every wheel selection through the active vanilla weapon row and the game's normal equip path.[/*]
[*]Leaves native weapon loadouts 1 through 3 untouched.[/*]
[*]Redirects the game's four configurable weapon-loadout actions to Glorious loadouts 1 through 4, preserving keyboard and controller rebinding.[/*]
[*]Provides dedicated configurable keys only for extended loadouts 5 and 6, defaulting to number keys 5 and 6.[/*]
[*]Mirrors the lower weapon sectors from the upper positions while keeping their item artwork upright.[/*]
[*]Can hide the center control diagram and controls legend and supports immediate left-click selection by default.[/*]
[*]Shows virtual-loadout-aware arrow quantities on every bow loadout.[/*]
[*]Lets a left click on a bow loadout's arrow icon cycle that loadout's arrow type without selecting it or closing the wheel.[/*]
[/list]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]One-Menu Equipping[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Left click equips a hovered weapon or spell to the main hand.[/*]
[*]Right click equips it to the off hand from the same picker.[/*]
[*]Applies the same configurable hand shortcuts to hovered weapons in the Bag.[/*]
[*]Supports both hand pickers, optional off-hand-to-main-hand redirection, equipped-item toggling, click interception, and short notifications.[/*]
[*]Requires no external OneMenuEquip plugin.[/*]
[/list]

If [b]owrocc.OneMenuEquip[/b] is still enabled, Glorious UI detects it and suppresses only its duplicate hooks for that session. Disable the old plugin to use Glorious UI's complete implementation and settings.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Smart Bag And Category Hotkeys[/size][/color][/heading]
The Smart Bag / All hotkey defaults to Tab. It first closes an active equipment or quick-slot picker, otherwise opens or navigates to Bag > All, then closes through the game's native Back hierarchy when All is already selected.

Optional direct hotkeys can open Weapons, Magic, Armor, Jewelry, Gems, Potions, Consumables, Crafting, Readables, Recipes, Quest Items, or Other. When the same physical key also triggers the generic Character Sheet action, Glorious UI suppresses that duplicate press so the Bag cannot immediately reopen. The separate Equipment action remains available.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Per-Save Equipment Persistence[/size][/color][/heading]
Glorious UI stores six virtual quick slots, six weapon loadouts, and the selected weapon loadout in [b]GloriousEquipmentSlots.dat[/b] inside the active game save. It also keeps a per-save local backup under:

[code]
BepInEx/config/GloriousUI/Equipment/
[/code]

Weapon assignments use template GUIDs. When several copies match, Glorious UI chooses the highest-level and then lightest copy while keeping dual-wielded copies distinct. After loading, it restores the selected weapon loadout through the normal native equipment path.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

Automatically preserved: Hero HUD, Quick Slot HUD, arrow, Wyrd Power, and buff/debuff position and scale tuning; Hero HUD visible duration; Smart Bag, health potion, mana potion, loadout 5-6, and quick-slot 1-6 hotkeys. The visual-only buff/debuff layout test is permanently excluded.

Glorious UI replaces the previous Better Quick Slots and Wyrdframe identities with a new plugin GUID, DLL, package, config, and persistence names. It does not import settings or save data from those previous identities. Disable or remove the old plugins before enabling Glorious UI.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Compatibility[/size][/color][/heading]
[b]Eyes in the Dark:[/b] Optional soft dependency. Eyes creates and controls its Wyrd Threat meter and Wyrdnight rest availability. Glorious UI requests below-resource-bars meter placement and independently controls only the rest clock and time presentation.

Do not load these older plugins alongside Glorious UI:

[list]
[*][b]owrocc.ModifyHeroHUD.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]owrocc.ModifyQuickSlotsHud.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]owrocc.HideLevelUp.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]owrocc.MoreWeaponSlots.dll[/b] / More Weapon Loadouts[/*]
[*][b]owrocc.OneMenuEquip.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]owrocc.RebindQuickWheel.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]owrocc.BagHotkeys.dll[/b] / Bag Category Hotkeys[/*]
[/list]

[b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/169]Immersive HUD[/url]:[/b] Partially compatible. Keep [b]ForceVanillaHeroHud=false[/b]. Its bar and quick-slot visibility settings may still cause uneven or delayed fading.

[b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/111]Better UI[/url]:[/b] Compatible outside its Quick Wheel replacements. When Glorious UI's six-loadout wheel is enabled, set these Better UI options to false because Glorious UI removes the consumable wheel slots and supplies virtual-loadout-aware ammo counters and arrow cycling:

[code]
QuickSlotEffectEnabled = false
AmmoCounterEnabled = false
ArrowCycleEnabled = false
[/code]

[b]Optional diagnostics:[/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] can show critical load errors in-game, with details in the BepInEx log.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Troubleshooting[/size][/color][/heading]
If HUD elements appear twice or fight for position, remove the older plugins listed above and verify that another mod is not controlling the same Hero HUD, quick-slot, status-icon, compass, notification, arrow, Wyrd Power, Equipment, or quick-use component.

If Better UI is installed and the wheel shows overlapping consumable effects or ammo controls, confirm that its three compatibility settings are disabled. If an individual Glorious UI feature causes trouble, disable that feature's ownership setting to restore the corresponding vanilla control while testing.

Enable [b]Diagnostics[/b] only while investigating behavior. Full details are written to [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b].

Version 0.1.4
Raised ConfigSchemaVersion from 1 to 2 because the default Volume changed from 0.45 to 0.40; untouched old defaults now regenerate at 0.40 while compatible customized settings remain eligible for conservative recovery.

Version 0.1.3
Updated the successful startup message to describe replaceable custom slide audio instead of referring to the included Foley as placeholders.

Version 0.1.2
Reworked puddle loops and transients as a 70 percent ground and 30 percent water blend so wet slides retain clear ground friction instead of sounding like stream wading.
Lowered the default overall slide-audio volume from 0.45 to 0.40. Existing saved configurations retain their current value.

Version 0.1.1
Replaced the synthetic development placeholders with 66 edited terrain Foley assets from the licensed Footsteps: Full Bundle.
Added two seamless loop, start, and stop variants for each of the eleven supported terrain categories.
Standardized runtime audio as mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM with controlled loudness, peak headroom, and measured loop boundaries.

Version 0.1.0
Added terrain-aware FMOD slide audio for Better Movement slide begin, continuation, terrain transitions, cancellation, and ending.
Added native terrain splatmap, mesh surface, and shallow-water detection without patching Immersive Footsteps' playback route.
Added replaceable loop, start, and stop WAV discovery for eleven native terrain categories.
Added lightweight synthetic placeholder audio for development testing while final licensed Foley is selected.
Added configurable volume, speed response, pitch response, crossfade timing, attenuation distance, terrain polling, and diagnostics.

Processing record

The source scuffs were trimmed, resampled to 48 kHz, DC corrected, terrain-band filtered, level matched, overlapped into sustained texture beds, and crossfaded at measured low-discontinuity loop boundaries. Start and stop transients were selected separately and shaped with short fades. Runtime exports use mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM with TPDF dither. Review masters use mono 48 kHz 32-bit float.

Generated review records are kept under .codex-temp/slide-audio-build/source-manifest.json and source-manifest.tsv. Those records map every output to its exact source files and measured loudness, peak, duration, and loop-boundary values.

[center][size=5][b]KS Better Movement Addon[/b][/size]
[i]Terrain-aware sliding audio for Better Movement[/i][/center]

[b]Overview[/b]

KS Better Movement Addon gives [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/102]Better Movement[/url] slides a positional surface layer. Dirt scrapes like dirt, gravel scatters like gravel, puddles splash, and long downhill slides can change sound as the player crosses onto another terrain.

The addon follows the game's real slide lifecycle. It starts with the slide, continues for Better Movement's extended downhill slides, crossfades between surfaces, and stops when the slide ends or is cancelled.

[b]Features[/b]

[list]
[*]Terrain-aware slide loops for all native hero footstep surface families.[/*]
[*]Native terrain splatmap, explicit mesh-surface, and shallow-water detection.[/*]
[*]Positional 3D FMOD playback routed through the game's SFX mixer when available.[/*]
[*]Speed-responsive volume and pitch.[/*]
[*]Smooth start, stop, and surface-transition fades.[/*]
[*]Replaceable loop, start, and stop WAV folders with random variation support.[/*]
[*]Safe ground fallback when a custom surface folder is missing.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Included audio[/b]

The addon includes two edited loop, start, and stop variations for each supported terrain family. Runtime files are mono 48 kHz PCM WAVs prepared for positional playback, consistent level, and clean looping.

[b]Audio source:[/b] Edited material from [url=https://www.asoundeffect.com/sound-library/footsteps-full-bundle/]Footsteps: Full Bundle[/url] by Sound Armoury / Matt J Hart, used under license.

[b]Compatibility[/b]

[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/257]Immersive Footsteps[/url] remains compatible. This addon does not patch its protected footstep playback route and does not extract or redistribute its audio. Immersive Footsteps handles ordinary steps; KS Better Movement Addon supplies the continuous terrain layer during slides.

[b]Requirements[/b]

[list]
[*]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon Mono branch[/*]
[*]BepInEx 5 Mono[/*]
[*]Better Movement 1.3.0 or newer[/*]
[/list]

[b]Configuration[/b]

Settings are generated at:

[code]BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.better-movement-addon.cfg[/code]

Volume, speed response, pitch response, crossfade timing, positional distance, terrain polling, and diagnostics are configurable.

[b]Custom audio[/b]

Surface folders live under:

[code]BetterMovementAddon/audio/slide/<surface>/[/code]

WAV filenames must contain [code]_loop_[/code], [code]_start_[/code], or [code]_stop_[/code]. At least one seamless loop is required for a directly supported surface. Multiple variations are selected randomly.

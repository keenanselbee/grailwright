Version 1.1.0
Companion addon for TG All Lights Cast Shadows 1.2.0.
Restores Unity global shadow-quality state after the parent mod disables nearby light upgrades.
Shields configured bonfire and campfire light objects from being upgraded into shadow-casting lights.

Improved stable light selection with exact light-range sphere tests, a configurable retention advantage, and moderate screen-centre priority.

Initial shadows now arrive in batches of four. Later replacements use one sequential 0.6-second handoff that fades the outgoing shadow to zero, restores it, and then fades the incoming shadow into the same slot without overlapping their shadow maps.

Added an independent vanilla HeroLight exclusion while retaining stronger MageLight and No Player Light ownership integration and active MageLight face reservation.

Added tightening-only interior limits for light count, distance, shadow-map faces, and promoted resolution. Their defaults match the permanent profile, and they can never raise a stricter permanent, parent, or combat limit.

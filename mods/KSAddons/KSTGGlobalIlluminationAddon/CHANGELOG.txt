Version 0.1.0
Added contextual Full, Balanced, and Performance profiles for Global Illumination.
Uses the parent mod's current settings unchanged for Full, starts interiors at Full, and starts exteriors at Balanced.
Added a conservative 60 FPS adaptive controller with smoothed gameplay sampling, sustained thresholds, cooldowns, scene warmup, and session-only scene memory.
Balanced preserves indirect-light multipliers while capping samples at 4, bounces at 1, and using half-resolution screen-space GI.
Performance temporarily disables screen-space GI while preserving the parent mod's indirect diffuse and reflection multipliers.
Pauses performance decisions during non-gameplay conditions and restores the parent profile when disabled, unloaded, or outside active gameplay.
Keeps All Lights Cast Shadows behavior entirely separate.

Version 1.2.8
Reduced the default quest-giver outline distance from 20 to 15 metres, story-scan frame budget from 5 to 1 millisecond, outline-bake frame budget from 1.5 to 0.25 milliseconds, and animated outline refresh rate from 30 to 10 updates per second.

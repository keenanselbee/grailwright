Version 0.1.3
Added ConfigSchemaVersion 1 so older or unversioned configs are backed up and regenerated with clean defaults.
Kept loadout duplicate-entry and input-redirect behavior unchanged.

Version 0.1.2
Fixed duplicate slot activation when More Weapon Loadouts slots 1-4 and the vanilla loadout actions share Alpha1-Alpha4.
Keeps the vanilla action suppressed while allowing More Weapon Loadouts to handle its configured shortcut once.
Preserves redirect support for controller, rebound, and other vanilla loadout inputs without a matching MWS shortcut press.

Version 0.1.1
Companion addon for More Weapon Loadouts 1.5.0 from Owrocc HUD Tweaks file 60.2.
Handles duplicate VirtualLoadouts.json read behavior so useful/latest slot data is preferred over stale empty entries.
Redirects vanilla gameplay weapon loadout keys into More Weapon Loadouts virtual slots by default.

Version 1.2.2
Added ConfigSchemaVersion 1 so older or unversioned configs are backed up and regenerated with clean defaults.
Kept Wyrd Scent meter hiding, randomized hunts, and mixed-pack behavior unchanged.

Version 1.2.1
Allowed Wyrdspirit to appear as a prioritized mixed-pack sidecar.
Added a special Wyrdspirit sidecar rule: when selected, Wyrdspirit spawns as a random 1-4 spirit cluster.
Reweighted sidecars so weaker enemies are favored while same-tier and same-family picks are much less likely.

Version 1.2.0
Added optional mixed hunt packs for randomized Stalking, Hunter, Pursuit, and Marked hunts.
Extra pack mates are selected from the same hunt layer, must be same-or-lower tier than the primary target, and avoid duplicate templates.
Contact hunts remain single-only, hard pack mates are disabled by default, and extras are pressure enemies rather than official hunt objectives.

Version 1.1.1
Added optional Wyrd Scent meter hiding for title, loading, startup, and other non-hero scenes.
Kept existing safe-from-Wyrdness hiding, meter offsets, and Wyrd Hunt spawn randomization behavior unchanged.

Version 1.1.0
Companion addon for Wyrd Hunt 0.4.65.
Preserves HUD tweaks that hide the Wyrd Scent meter when safe from Wyrdness and reposition the meter with offsets.
Adds configurable weighted random native hunt spawns so the active hunt target matches the monster that actually spawns.
Keeps settings in ks.tgfoa.wyrd-hunt-addon.cfg and is intended as a companion tuning addon for the parent mod.

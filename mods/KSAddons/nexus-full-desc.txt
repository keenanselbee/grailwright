[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]All Lights Cast Shadows Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/133]All Lights Cast Shadows[/url] that preserves its rich shadowed-light experience while managing nearby lights through stable, view-aware performance limits. It reduces flicker, shadow popping, and sudden performance spikes, protects special and player-owned lighting, and restores every value it changes.

[list]
[*][b]Performance-safe limits:[/b] Keeps up to 16 nearby lights within 25 metres and a 48-face estimated shadow budget by default. Stricter limits from the original mod or the combat settings still win.[/*]
[*][b]Stable lighting while moving:[/b] Uses each light's exact range sphere, favours useful sources near the centre of the view, retains good existing choices, and transfers one replacement shadow at a time through a zero-strength handoff. Initial shadows arrive in small batches instead of all at once.[/*]
[*][b]Protected special lighting:[/b] Leaves the vanilla HeroLight, Wyrd Sight highlights, summons, interface previews, lockpicking, the portable Bonfire, configured firelights, and optional user exclusions under their original lighting systems.[/*]
[*][b]Player-light compatibility:[/b] Leaves the player light entirely under MageLight or No Player Light when either is installed. If both are present, the addon warns that MageLight can reactivate the light disabled by No Player Light.[/*]
[*][b]Optional interior tuning:[/b] Provides separate tightening-only interior ceilings for light count, distance, estimated faces, and shadow resolution. Their defaults match the permanent limits and can never raise a stricter parent, permanent, or combat value.[/*]
[*][b]Optional dawn and dusk improvement:[/b] Can shorten the weak-shadow transition around sunrise and sunset without adding another light or shadow map. It is off by default and can stay consistent with the changing world-clock speed from Eyes in the Dark.[/*]
[*][b]Reversible performance controls:[/b] Suppresses added volumetric shadows by default, caps promoted shadow resolution without raising lower authored values, and lowers the cap during outdoor combat. Every lighting value changed by the addon is restored when released.[/*]
[*][b]Clean parent integration and feedback:[/b] Keeps the original mod's toggle and shadow-strength settings, never edits its JSON, and supports toggle notifications, detailed diagnostics, Grail Floating Text summaries, and previous-settings recovery.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Architecture credit:[/b] The safe-selector, bounded-ownership, semantic-exclusion, restoration, exact range-sphere ranking, staged activation, and sequential handoff design was adapted from TGAllLightsCastShadowsSafe by [url=https://forums.nexusmods.com/profile/194045963-pupkidze007/]pupkidze007[/url].

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.tg-all-lights-cast-shadows-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Better Movement Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/102]Better Movement[/url] 1.3.0 or newer that gives sliding responsive, terrain-matched 3D audio and configurable behavior during combat. Slides transition naturally across eleven surface families, while combat modes can retain, soften, or suspend Better Movement without rewriting its settings.

[list]
[*][b]Terrain-aware slide audio:[/b] Adds continuous sounds to ordinary and extended downhill slides across eleven terrain, floor, and shallow-water surface families.[/*]
[*][b]Natural slide transitions:[/b] Uses start, loop, and stop sounds, crossfades when the surface changes, and adjusts volume and pitch with sliding speed.[/*]
[*][b]Flexible combat behavior:[/b] Keep Better Movement unchanged, retain half of its movement-speed bonuses, smoothly remove those bonuses, restore vanilla mobility rules, or suppress every Better Movement feature during combat. Changes blend over one second, while Full Vanilla waits for active movement actions to finish and safely handles the original mod's separate stamina and FOV changes without rewriting its config.[/*]
[*][b]3D and customizable audio:[/b] Plays sounds from the player's position and supports replaceable WAV folders for custom sound packs.[/*]
[*][b]Immersive Footsteps compatibility:[/b] Keeps regular footsteps from [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/257]Immersive Footsteps[/url] separate from continuous slide audio.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Audio source:[/b] Edited material from [url=https://www.asoundeffect.com/sound-library/footsteps-full-bundle/]Footsteps: Full Bundle[/url] by Sound Armoury / Matt J Hart, used under license.

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.better-movement-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Better Volumetric Fog Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/145]Better Volumetric Fog[/url] that improves performance and focuses its cleaner volumetric lighting where it matters most. It uses efficient cached fog discovery, enhances verified interiors by default, and restores vanilla volumetrics outdoors, during loading, and whenever the enhancement is inactive.

[list]
[*][b]Smoother fog discovery:[/b] Replaces the original mod's repeated search through every loaded Fog object with a cache that is seeded once and updated by HDRP volume events.[/*]
[*][b]Interior focus:[/b] Applies the cleaner volumetrics only in verified interiors by default, where blocky lamp, candle, and window light shafts are most noticeable.[/*]
[*][b]Balanced default quality:[/b] Uses Low indoors for 30 percent screen resolution and 32 slices, then restores vanilla volumetrics outdoors, during loading, and outside active gameplay.[/*]
[*][b]Exact restoration:[/b] Preserves the original mod's own backups so the game's Fog values and override states return exactly when the enhancement is inactive.[/*]
[*][b]Clean parent integration and feedback:[/b] Keeps the original hotkey and JSON reload behavior, never edits TGVolumetricFix.json, and allows optional global operation or higher quality through this addon's config. Grail Floating Text can confirm actual parent toggle changes without announcing routine interior and exterior transitions.[/*]
[/list]

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.tg-volumetric-fix-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Contact Shadows Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/135]Contact Shadows[/url] that concentrates stronger local shadow detail around the nearby interior lights that benefit from it most. It maintains a stable four-light budget by default, avoids repeated scene-wide searches, and fully restores every touched light and camera when inactive.

[list]
[*][b]Interior focus:[/b] Enables contact shadows only in interiors by default, where nearby local lighting provides the strongest visual improvement.[/*]
[*][b]Stable four-light budget:[/b] Keeps up to four nearby point or spot lights active by default, holds valid selections briefly, and requires a stronger challenger before replacing the weakest selection. This improves interior coverage without returning to the parent mod's broad light activation.[/*]
[*][b]Conservative visuals:[/b] Uses a shorter ray, softer opacity, 20-meter visual range, and 16 samples by default. Eight samples remains available as a lighter manual alternative.[/*]
[*][b]Lower scan overhead:[/b] Ignores inactive, disabled, directional, area, zero-intensity, and zero-range lights. It selects from a cached active-light list instead of repeatedly scanning inactive scene objects.[/*]
[*][b]Exact restoration and feedback:[/b] Restores every selected light and touched camera to their original HDRP values, removes the parent volume while inactive, and never edits the parent JSON file. Grail Floating Text can confirm actual parent toggle changes without announcing routine interior and exterior transitions.[/*]
[/list]

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.tg-contact-shadows-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Global Illumination Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/134]Global Illumination[/url] that adapts lighting quality to sustained performance instead of forcing every scene to carry the same cost. It starts conservatively, adjusts one tier at a time with separate indoor and outdoor limits, preserves the original brightness, and restores the parent mod's settings when inactive.

[list]
[*][b]Performance-first startup:[/b] New scenes start at Performance by default, then raise quality only after sustained FPS meets the target.[/*]
[*][b]Indoor and outdoor presets:[/b] Uses separate recovery limits for interiors and exteriors. Defaults can recover to the original lighting indoors and Balanced outdoors.[/*]
[*][b]Balanced outdoor performance:[/b] Uses lighter outdoor lighting settings without weakening the original mod's indirect lighting.[/*]
[*][b]Gradual automatic adjustment:[/b] Raises or lowers quality one tier at a time after sustained performance, and can remember the last successful scene tier for the current session.[/*]
[*][b]Safe Performance fallback:[/b] Temporarily disables the most demanding lighting effect while preserving the original brightness. It pauses adjustments during loading, menus, transitions, and unfocused play, never edits the parent JSON, and restores parent values when inactive.[/*]
[*][b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] feedback:[/b] Confirms actual parent toggle changes independently. The default-on ShowGrailFloatingTextDiagnostics switch controls adaptive-tier notices with the new tier and smoothed FPS, and remains inactive unless Diagnostics is enabled.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Recommended mod:[/b] Use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/213]Light Control[/url].

[list]
[*][b]Dungeons:[/b] World Intensity Day/Night = 2; Brightness Offset Day/Night = -2.[/*]
[*][b]Interiors:[/b] World Intensity Day/Night = 1.5; Brightness Offset Day/Night = -1.5.[/*]
[/list]

If interiors are still too dark, raise World Intensity and/or Brightness Offset.

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.tg-global-illumination-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Persistent Corpses Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/228]Persistent Corpses[/url] that makes restored bodies settle naturally and gives you control over how many corpses remain in the world. It conceals unstable ragdolls until they are ready, offers Limited, All, and Vanilla retention modes, and performs cautious long-rest cleanup while preserving visible loot.

[list]
[*][b]Smooth corpse restoration:[/b] Restored bodies can briefly appear standing and then fall over. The addon conceals them while ragdoll physics settles, then reveals them in a natural resting pose.[/*]
[*][b]Careful settling:[/b] Settling pauses during loading screens and paused gameplay, includes a timeout for unstable bodies, and stops checking each corpse once it is ready.[/*]
[*][b]Limited retention (default):[/b] Keeps up to 10 complete corpses loaded. Extra bodies use the game's normal cleanup once they are far enough away, avoiding visible changes near the player.[/*]
[*][b]Alternative retention modes:[/b] [b]All[/b] preserves every corpse, while [b]Vanilla[/b] allows all eligible distant corpses to use normal cleanup.[/*]
[*][b]Safe long-rest cleanup:[/b] After a bonfire rest of at least six hours, empty corpses are gradually removed and bodies with loot are replaced with simpler versions. Visible loot is preserved, unsafe bodies remain untouched, and no additional save data is written.[/*]
[/list]

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.persistent-corpses-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Wyrd Sight Addon[/size][/color][/heading]
Companion addon for [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/94]Wyrd Sight[/url] that turns highlighting into extendable timed pulses and can reveal eligible quest givers. It respects progression locks, spreads background work out to reduce stutter, and immediately removes its outlines and rendering resources when disabled.

[list]
[*][b]Timed Wyrd Sight pulses:[/b] Changes the Highlight Key from a toggle into a timed pulse. Repeated presses extend the pulse, and the addon never turns off Wyrd Sight activated by something else.[/*]
[*][b]Quest-giver highlighting:[/b] Adds optional, performance-friendly quest-giver outlines inspired by Avalon Untold, which is not required. Progression-locked offers stay hidden without treating temporary conditions as permanent blockers.[/*]
[*][b]Adjustable behavior:[/b] Pulse duration, retry timing, quest range, and performance limits remain configurable. For a clean pulse, use [b]Enable Glow Shape = true[/b] and [b]Enable Wyrd Sight Particles = false[/b] in Wyrd Sight.[/*]
[*][b]Smooth background processing:[/b] Processes quest data in the background and spreads scans and outline updates out to reduce stutter. Quest availability refreshes with each pulse and every 60 seconds while Wyrd Sight stays active.[/*]
[*][b]Clean disable and re-enable:[/b] Turning off Enabled or HighlightQuestGivers immediately removes its outlines and rendering resources. Cached quest data can be reused if the feature is enabled again.[/*]
[/list]

Config file:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.wyrd-sight-addon.cfg[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
Install with Vortex as [b]BepInEx plugins[/b], or place the selected addon folders under:

[code]
BepInEx/plugins/TGAllLightsCastShadowsAddon/TGAllLightsCastShadowsAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/BetterMovementAddon/BetterMovementAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/TGVolumetricFixAddon/TGVolumetricFixAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/TGContactShadowsAddon/TGContactShadowsAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/TGGlobalIlluminationAddon/TGGlobalIlluminationAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/PersistentCorpsesAddon/PersistentCorpsesAddon.dll
BepInEx/plugins/WyrdSightAddon/WyrdSightAddon.dll[/code]

Keep each addon's original mod installed. All seven addons declare hard dependencies, so they will not run without their original mods.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Each addon manages its own config and backups.

Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Version Safety[/size][/color][/heading]
These addons touch specific internals of their original mods. The listed original mod versions are the versions this bundle was built against and tested with.

Some Nexus file versions differ from the internal BepInEx plugin versions shown in the game log. When that happens, this page lists both labels.

Later original mod updates may make an addon unnecessary, partially incompatible, or fully incompatible. If an original mod updates, check its changelog, keep backups, and disable the matching addon if the original mod includes the same behavior natively or its internals change.

[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Killing Blow Mastery[/size][/color]
[i]Weapon-mastery Executions, finisher sounds, and proficiency XP for the skill that lands the kill.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Killing Blow Mastery[/b] turns combat executions into a weapon-mastery system. At 25 proficiency, aiming at an eligible enemy at 10% health or lower shows an [b]Execute[/b] prompt. Press your normal Interact key or control to perform the execution. As proficiency with the selected weapon rises, the prompt becomes available at progressively higher health, reaching 25% at proficiency 100.

It also gives a small extra combat proficiency reward to the skill that actually lands an enemy's killing blow.

The goal is to make side weapons, shields, archery, and magic less painful to train without turning last-hitting into a replacement for normal combat practice. Soften an enemy with your main setup, swap to the weapon or spell you want to train, and earn a modest finishing bonus if that source lands the kill.

It also adds optional finisher feedback: a shared [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] reward message and category-based killing blow sounds for blades, axes, blunt weapons, bows, shields, unarmed attacks, and several magic types.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Combat Finishers and Executions[/size][/color][/heading]
[b]AutomaticCombatFinishersEnabled[/b] controls the killing-blow animations the game starts automatically during ordinary melee attacks. It is enabled by default.

[b]CombatExecutionMode[/b] controls the separate interaction available while aiming at a living enemy:
[list]
[*][b]Vanilla:[/b] Preserve the game's normal combat execution rules.[/*]
[*][b]Execution:[/b] Unlock [b]Execute[/b] through melee-weapon proficiency, then widen its low-health window as mastery rises.[/*]
[*][b]Off:[/b] Remove only the living-enemy combat execution interaction.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Executions are a progression system, not an instant low-level power.[/b] They unlock at 25 proficiency with the weapon that supplies the selected animation. From there, their health window grows linearly:

[code]
| Weapon proficiency | Execution window |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Below 25           | Locked           |
| 25                 | 10% health       |
| 50                 | 15% health       |
| 75                 | 20% health       |
| 100                | 25% health       |
[/code]

KBM follows the weapon the game actually selects. Main-hand, off-hand, and normal-finisher fallback animations use their own effective proficiency, and the Versatile Weapons integration respects the current One-Handed or Two-Handed grip. A high-level weapon in the other hand cannot authorize a low-level weapon's Execution.

The game's normal distance, kill-prevention, and external-death safeguards remain active. After those checks pass, KBM first selects from the equipped melee weapon's loaded execution animations, then falls back to its loaded normal finisher animations when the execution list has no playable hero animation. The game's situational direction, stagger, and random-chance filters do not block the prompt. Loaded animation assets and the native target-template restriction still apply. Executions use the existing finisher animation system and still count as killing blows for this mod's normal rewards.

[b]ExpandedExecutionTargets[/b] is enabled by default, letting additional hostile enemy templates attempt the shipped humanoid execution animations after applying [b]ExpandedExecutionExcludedAbstracts[/b]. That editable exact-match list defaults to [b]Animal;Animal_Prey[/b], preventing wolves, bears, wildlife, and other animal-classified rigs from attempting badly aligned humanoid animations. Disable expanded targets for the native humanoid restriction, remove a family name to allow it, or clear the list to allow every expanded target. The setting description lists all known game abstract families for reference.

KBM Executions suppress the selected execution asset's slow-motion flag only during their scoped start and restore its exact original value immediately afterward. KBM does not add visual or audio slow motion. Ordinary automatic kill cams remain untouched and retain the game's native slow motion and audio.

With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/241]Steel and Bone[/url] 4.0.1 and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url] 3.6.2, the Execute prompt can display the target's authoritative Meager, Worthy, Potent, or Prime skull in white. The same shape follows the real normalized finisher progress toward dark red, then hands exact target-matched feedback to the normal layered killing-blow marker near completion. A cancelled finisher never presents a false kill.

These settings do not alter the separate hold-interact story execution used for important unconscious NPCs. Disable automatic combat finishers and set combat execution mode to Off to remove combat finisher animations while leaving those story executions intact.

When [b]Diagnostics[/b] is enabled, throttled per-target log entries identify selected-weapon proficiency and its calculated health threshold, health and safety rejections, missing execution lists, native animation-selection failures, the native activation delay, and successful Execute-prompt availability. Every combat finisher, including automatic vanilla kill cams, also reports its origin, target, native slowdown flag, normalized progress, real elapsed time, gameplay time scale, attached slowdown-handle count, native cleanup, and OnExit result. A warning identifies a native Finisher slowdown that remains attached near zero time scale for six real-time seconds. [b]FullPotencyExecutions[/b] can temporarily test Execution eligibility at proficiency 100 and the configured mastery health threshold without changing proficiency, rewards, or save data; it applies only while Diagnostics is enabled.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Finisher Sounds[/size][/color][/heading]
The default sound mode is [b]WeaponSpecific[/b]. The mod tries to play a matching category sound for the kill source, then uses FMOD for playback with optional no-repeat selection, subtle pitch randomization, and distance-based volume fade.

[code]
| Mode           | Sound style       |
|----------------|-------------------|
| WeaponSpecific | category pools    |
| Soulslike      | killing_blow1.wav |
| GoatTest       | goat.wav          |
| Off            | silent            |
[/code]

[b]FinisherSoundRangeVolume[/b] applies to all active sound modes, including [b]WeaponSpecific[/b], [b]Soulslike[/b], and [b]GoatTest[/b]. Set it to 0 to disable distance fade. The default 1 uses the full 0m = 100%, 30m+ = 10% curve:

[code]
| Distance | Volume |
|----------|--------|
| 0m       | 100%   |
| 7.5m     | 78%    |
| 15m      | 55%    |
| 22.5m    | 33%    |
| 30m+     | 10%    |
[/code]

If FMOD playback fails, the mod falls back to the older Unity AudioSource path and logs the failure when Diagnostics is enabled.

[b]UseNonCorporealEnemySounds[/b] is enabled by default. Matched non-corporeal targets use [b]non_corporeal1.wav[/b] through [b]non_corporeal5.wav[/b] only, overriding weapon, magic, Soulslike, and [b]_dry[/b] routing for those targets. [b]GoatTest[/b] still uses [b]goat.wav[/b] for diagnostics, and [b]Off[/b] remains silent.

[b]UseBloodlessSoundVariants[/b] is enabled by default. Targets whose names, templates, or type text match [b]BloodlessSoundBlacklistTerms[/b] try matching [b]_dry[/b] files before their normal pool sound. [b]BloodlessSoundWhitelistTerms[/b] can force normal sounds for specific targets. This only changes sound routing; it does not change XP or kill eligibility.

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Sound Routing[/size][/color][/heading]
Classified kills use their specific pool by default. That keeps, for example, a two-handed axe kill from falling through to the general killing_blow pool unless you explicitly enable that fallback.

[code]
| Group      | Kill type             | Sound pool        |
|------------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| Target     | matched non-corporeal | non_corporeal     |
| One-Handed | blunt                 | one_handed_blunt  |
| One-Handed | axe                   | one_handed_axe    |
| One-Handed | blade/dagger/sickle   | one_handed_blade  |
| One-Handed | unknown subtype       | one_handed_blade  |
| Two-Handed | blunt                 | two_handed_blunt  |
| Two-Handed | axe                   | two_handed_axe    |
| Two-Handed | blade/polearm         | two_handed_blade  |
| Two-Handed | unknown subtype       | two_handed_blade  |
| Archery    | short bow             | archery_short     |
| Archery    | medium/unknown bow    | archery_medium    |
| Archery    | heavy bow             | archery_heavy     |
| Shield     | bash                  | shield_bash       |
| Unarmed    | unarmed               | unarmed           |
| Magic      | blood                 | magic_blood       |
| Magic      | fire                  | magic_fire        |
| Magic      | frost                 | magic_frost       |
| Magic      | poison                | magic_poison      |
| Magic      | electric              | magic_electric    |
| Magic      | wyrdness              | magic_wyrdness    |
| Magic      | water                 | magic_water       |
| Magic      | other                 | magic_arcane      |
| Other      | unclassified eligible | killing_blow      |
[/code]

Broad [b]one_handed[/b], [b]two_handed[/b], [b]archery[/b], [b]shield[/b], and [b]magic[/b] fallback pools are not used in this release.

For bloodless targets, each routed pool can have matching [b]_dry[/b] files. The dry file is tried before the normal file it replaces:

[code]
| Normal file           | Bloodless variant        |
|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| two_handed_blade1.wav | two_handed_blade1_dry.wav |
| magic_fire3.wav       | magic_fire3_dry.wav       |
| killing_blow1.wav     | killing_blow1_dry.wav     |
[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Notifications[/size][/color][/heading]
Notifications are on by default and show the exact bonus and skill.

[code]
Default text: Killing blow: +{xp} {skill}
Example:      Killing blow: +8 Two-Handed
[/code]

[b]NotificationMode[/b] defaults to [b]GrailFloatingText[/b], the optional shared [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url] overlay. With Grail Floating Text 2.3.1 or newer, recognized one-handed and two-handed kills use specific sword, axe, blunt, dagger, or spear icons while the awarded proficiency remains One-Handed or Two-Handed. Sickles use the One-Handed Axe icon while retaining blade-pool audio. Older GFT versions and unknown subtypes use the broad proficiency icon. Archery, Shield, Unarmed, and Magic continue to use their skill-level icons. With Grail Floating Text 1.4.7 or newer, killing-blow rewards use the [b]killing-blow[/b] event ID and are red by default through Grail Floating Text's editable [b]RedEvents[/b] group.

[b]NotificationTextFormat[/b] supports:

[code]
{xp}
{skill}
{enemy}
{weapon}
{enemyXP}
[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Updating[/size][/color][/heading]
Incompatible config updates create a backup and generate current defaults. Some supported settings are automatically carried over.

FoA Mod Manager's final [b]Import Previous Settings[/b] tab shows the current and available backup schemas. Toggle the import action to restore compatible customized settings. Restart the game after importing.

FoA Mod Manager organizes the controls into focused General, Combat Finishers, Weapon Skills, Notifications, Reward Audio, Advanced Audio Routing, Advanced, and Diagnostics sections. These display groups and clearer labels do not move the underlying config keys or reset customized values.

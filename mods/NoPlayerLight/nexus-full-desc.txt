[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]No Player Light[/size][/color]
[i]No config. No polling. No ongoing performance cost.[/i][/center]

[line]

No Player Light does one small thing: it removes the hidden player-centered [b]HeroLight[/b] from [b]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon[/b].

That means caves, nights, interiors, torches, lanterns, ReShade presets, and lighting mods get to carry the scene without a constant artificial glow around the player.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Simple By Design[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]No config file.[/*]
[*]No per-frame update loop.[/*]
[*]No background polling.[/*]
[*]No diagnostic scanning or log spam.[/*]
[*]No game files, bundles, scenes, world lights, weather, shadows, or post-processing edits.[/*]
[/list]

The plugin only checks for [b]HeroLight[/b] on startup and during a few short scene-load retries. If it finds the object, it disables it. After that retry window, it stops doing work until the next scene load.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
Install with Vortex as a [b]BepInEx plugin[/b], or place the plugin folder here:

[code]
BepInEx/plugins/NoPlayerLight/NoPlayerLight.dll[/code]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Requirements[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon on the Mono branch.[/*]
[*]BepInEx 5 for the Mono build.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Want More Control?[/size][/color][/heading]
No Player Light is intentionally minimal. If you want more lighting controls, use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/229]Player Inner Light Control[/url].

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Notes[/size][/color][/heading]
This mod does not rebalance lighting. If an area becomes too dark, use a torch, lantern, ReShade adjustment, or lighting mod. No Player Light only removes the player-centered helper light.

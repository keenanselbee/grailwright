Light Soul Rend now preserves the same active servant after severing Empowerment or removing Soulforged ranks across the native summon, NPC, and Health-removal paths. Newly raised ordinary servants begin with a tighter preset curve: Soul Famine uses x0.85 starting Health, Grave Pact x1.00, and Dominion x1.25. Diagnostics now reports starting Health as a percentage and gains a one-shot action that promotes every currently active owned summon below a selected Soulforged rank to that genuine saved rank, making normal rank stripping easy to test.

[center][color=#38A840][size=6]Soul and Service[/size][/color]
[i]Bind the fallen. Command the dead. Bend every soul to your service.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Soul and Service - Summon Overhaul[/b] transforms the vanilla Soul Salvage spell into [b]Soul Rend[/b], the heart of a complete necromancer playstyle. Defeat enemies, judge the value of their remains, and choose whether to harvest their souls or bind them into your growing host.

Soul Rend can harvest corpses, damage and claim living enemies, raise the fallen, unbind servants for Mana and Soul Vigor, and Empower the worthy. The souls you harvest build [b]Soul Vigor[/b], which becomes [b]Necromantic Power[/b] and transforms a novice summoner into the master of a larger, stronger, more disciplined army.

Your servants are no longer disposable bodies that wander, spin, block doorways, or disappear behind you. Order them to attack, hold ground, form around you, change their behavior, regroup from anywhere, and surge forward as a Swarm. Keep proven servants alive and their victories permanently forge them into stronger, larger, brighter champions.

[b]For the full experience, these companion mods are highly recommended:[/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/223]Dishonored Dynamic Crosshair[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/277]Deeds of Avalon[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/247]Grail Floating Text[/url], and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/taintedgrailthefallofavalon/mods/252]Battlecry Voice Tuner[/url]. Together they add purpose-built reticles for corpse quality, servant commands, and confirmation pulses; track your necromantic progression; surface binding, summon-cost, reward, and command feedback in the world; and give your orders a voice.

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Quick Start Guide[/size][/color][/heading]

[b][color=#ffffff]1. Harvest Soul Vigor[/color][/b]

Light-cast Soul Rend on an eligible hostile corpse. Stronger corpses provide more Vigor, raising Necromantic Power and unlocking commands, capacity, and stronger servants.

[b][color=#ffffff]2. Raise the Fallen[/color][/b]

Heavy-cast Soul Rend on a corpse to overcome its binding resistance. Failed attempts preserve progress, and Soul Vigor is spent only when binding succeeds. Ordinary summon spells also cost Vigor.

[b][color=#ffffff]3. Command the Host[/color][/b]

Use Sprint with Interact to Attack, Hold, Follow, or change behavior. Guard protects you, Bulwark forms a defensive screen, and Hunt aggressively seeks enemies. Sprint + Take All controls or Recalls the whole host.

[b][color=#ffffff]4. Sustain Your Servants[/color][/b]

At low Power, necromantic upkeep steadily drains servant Health. Heavy Soul Rend restores them; at Power 100, a healthy servant can be permanently Empowered by spending twice its base Soul Vigor value at the current Power-scaled rate. From Power 40 onward, damage dealt also earns permanent Soulforged ranks.

[b][color=#ffffff]5. Spend and Rebuild[/color][/b]

Summoning and Empowering spend Soul Vigor and can lower Necromantic Power enough to relock abilities. Existing servants and completed Empower rituals are never undone, so harvest more souls to restore your mastery.

[code]
| Input                                             | Command                         |
|---------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Sprint + aim enemy + tap Interact                 | Attack / Swarm                  |
| Sprint + aim servant + tap Interact               | Hold / Follow                   |
| Sprint + Interact over empty space for 0.45s      | Change Behavior                 |
| Sprint + Take All; release after 0.45s            | Hold All / Follow All           |
| Sprint + Take All for 1.5s                        | Recall; Raise All with no host  |[/code]

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Soul Salvage becomes Soul Rend[/size][/color][/heading]

Soul Rend changes purpose according to its target and cast type:

[code]
| Target         | Light cast                         | Heavy cast                       |
|----------------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Corpse         | Harvest for Soul Vigor             | Bind and reanimate               |
| Owned servant  | Unbind for Mana and Soul Vigor     | Restore; Empower at 95% Health   |

This creates the central choice behind the mod: consume the dead for immediate progression, bind them into your host, or keep a proven servant alive long enough to Empower it. Claim a badly wounded enemy and it falls by your hand, then immediately rises into service without granting duplicate rewards.

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Soul Vigor Progression[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Soul Vigor[/b] is earned by harvesting suitable corpses and servants. It becomes [b]Necromantic Power[/b] through a smooth mastery curve: Power 100 marks full normal mastery at 1,000 Soul Vigor, while a long overmastery path reaches Power 200 at 5,000.

Soul Vigor remains stored with the active character and can continue rising, but gameplay Power stops at 200. It is an integer resource rather than a permanent score: summoning and Empowering spend it, immediately lower Necromantic Power, and can relock commands or future Empower rituals until enough is harvested again. Existing servants and completed Empower rituals are not undone merely because a milestone relocks. Soul Vigor is separate from Blood Magic Expansion's Blood Essence even though both progressions share the same mastery curve.

[code]
| Soul Vigor | Power | New mastery                                      |
|------------|-------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 0          | 0     | Reanimation and improved servant behavior        |
| 65         | 10    | Attack Command                                   |
| 133        | 20    | Individual Hold and Follow Commands              |
| 206        | 30    | Hold All and Follow All Commands                 |
| 369        | 50    | Guard and Hunt Behavior Command                  |
| 463        | 60    | Bulwark Behavior                                 |
| 567        | 70    | Recall Host Command                              |
| 826        | 90    | Swarm Attack Command                             |
| 1,000      | 100   | Empower and no necromantic upkeep                |
| 3,000      | 150   | Maximum Summon Capacity                          |
| 5,000      | 200   | Raise All; peak scaling                          |[/code]

These are command milestones, not sudden power jumps. Damage, resistance, raised health, binding strength, summon responsiveness, and upkeep improve smoothly between them.

[b]Summon Capacity[/b] lets mastery grow the host itself, adding +1 to the native summon limit at Power 50, +2 at Power 100, and +3 at Power 150. The configurable Summon Limit Bonus remains additional. Lowering the effective limit never destroys existing servants; it only prevents new summons until the host is below the current cap.

[code]
| Soul Vigor | Power | Summon damage | Damage taken | Raised starting health |
|------------|-------|---------------|--------------|------------------------|
| 0          | 0     | x0.75         | x1.25        | Random 40-60%          |
| 1,000      | 100   | x1.25         | x0.75        | Random 70-80%          |
| 5,000      | 200   | x1.50         | x0.50        | 100%                   |[/code]

Damage and resistance apply to every hero-owned summon, including ordinary summons from native spells. These progression values are multiplied by the active unlocked behavior: Guard adds 5% damage and 5% mitigation, Bulwark adds 15% mitigation, and Hunt adds 10% damage plus 10% pursuit/combat speed. Raised starting health applies only to enemies brought back through Soul Rend.

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Soulforged Servants[/size][/color][/heading]

At Power 40, servants can begin earning permanent [b]Soulforged ranks[/b] through damage actually dealt to hostile enemies. Rank I requires damage equal to twice that servant's original maximum Health. A new rank opens every 10 Power, with later ranks asking more of the servant and the final Soulforged XVII available at Power 200 after 54 Health-bars of service.

Damage progress is never wasted. A servant that has fought enough but lacks the next Power milestone waits at 100% until your mastery catches up, and earned ranks never fall when Soul Vigor is spent. Death, dismissal, or unbinding still ends that servant's personal legend.

Each rank adds 1% damage, 1% matching resistance, 1% visible size, and 1% Reanimation VFX brightness. These bonuses multiply with Empower rather than replacing it. Simply aim at a servant to see its name, rank, Health, and progress toward the next ascent; rank changes announce themselves without adding another command input.

For testing, [b]Diagnostics > Override Soulforged Rank[/b] can temporarily force the exact effective rank of every current and future servant. Disabling it restores each servant's real saved progress.

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Command Your Servants[/size][/color][/heading]

Every command uses remappable game actions and works without a crosshair mod. Sprint is the default modifier for targeted commands and always activates group commands, Recall, Raise All, and behavior cycling. Targeted Attack, Hold, and Follow can instead be configured without a modifier.

[code]
| Order             | Input                                         | Result                      |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Attack / Swarm    | Sprint + aim enemy + tap Interact             | Send host to target         |
| Directed Hunt     | In Hunt: Sprint + aim terrain + tap Interact  | Attack-move and sweep area  |
| Hold / Follow     | Sprint + aim servant + tap Interact           | Position or recall servant  |
| Hold / Follow All | Sprint + Take All; release at 0.45s           | Command entire formation    |
| Recall Host       | Sprint + Take All for 1.5s                    | Regroup host at hero        |
| Raise All         | No host: Sprint + Take All for 1.5s           | Raise corpses within 30m    |
| Change Behavior   | Sprint + Interact for 0.45s                   | Cycle available behaviors   |[/code]

Individual Hold and Follow remain quick taps by default. Enable [b]Hold Individual Formation Commands[/b] to require a deliberate 0.45-second Interact hold instead; releasing early cancels, while all other command inputs stay unchanged.

[b]Attack[/b] unlocks at Power 10 and sends the eligible host after one visible enemy across the configured Targeting Range. The order remains authoritative until the target is lost, replaced, or cancelled by Recall. At Power 90 it becomes [b]Swarm[/b]: for five seconds servants close up to 1.25x faster, and each deals 1.25x damage on its first successful hit. Repeated orders refresh rather than stack the bonus.

[b]Hold and Follow[/b] unlock for individual servants at Power 20 and the whole formation at Power 30. A held servant guards its position within an 8-meter leash; an explicit Attack temporarily releases it, after which it returns. Hold All and Follow All use the shorter Sprint + Take All release, while continuing the same hold to 1.5 seconds issues Recall once unlocked.

[b]Recall Host[/b] unlocks at Power 70. It breaks hold and attack orders, disengages the host for three seconds, and safely regroups every servant in distinct positions around the hero. Recall also clears stale combat locomotion and plays the green-dark summoning effect as each servant arrives.

[b]Raise All[/b] unlocks at Power 200. When no servants are alive, the Recall input raises viable hostile corpses within 30 meters, nearest first. Each corpse pays its current Soul Vigor cost, and the ritual stops at Summon Capacity or when the next corpse cannot be afforded.

[b]Behavior control[/b] unlocks Guard and Hunt at Power 50, followed by Bulwark at Power 60. Hold Sprint + Interact over empty space for 0.45 seconds to cycle the available behaviors. The selected behavior is stored with the character, inherited by new servants, and immediately reevaluates the host without overriding an explicit Attack or Swarm order. If spent Vigor relocks Bulwark, the host safely falls back to Guard until it returns.

[list]
[*][b]Guard[/b] is the balanced default. Servants deal 5% more damage, take 5% less, and protect the hero and host before proactively engaging visible faction-hostile enemies within 15 meters. They retain chosen enemies to 20 meters before returning to formation. Set Guard Engagement Range to zero for purely reactive bodyguards.[/*]
[*][b]Bulwark[/b] is the defensive screen. Servants take 15% less damage. Hold Sprint to drive a predictive Advance wall 4.5 meters ahead at 2x movement by default; release it to form a 3.5-meter Close Guard around the sides and rear, leaving a forward firing lane. Both stances engage nearby threats within 4 meters, retain them to 6, and return servants beyond an 8-meter leash. Distances, speed, and optional release grace are configurable, and explicit Attack or Swarm orders temporarily release the formation.[/*]
[*][b]Hunt[/b] turns the host into aggressive scouts. Servants deal 10% more damage, move 10% faster while pursuing or fighting, spread around a 5.5-meter perimeter, and seek visible faction-hostile enemies within 30 meters. For a deliberate attack-move, aim at reachable terrain at least 5 meters away and tap Interact while holding Sprint. Available hunters sweep the area, engage enemies they discover along the way, then return to ordinary Hunt if the search is empty.[/*]
[/list]

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Soul Rend Details[/size][/color][/heading]

Soul Rend changes with its target, turning every cast into a choice between immediate power, another body in the host, or the chance to claim a living foe.

[b]Light cast - Harvest or Rend[/b]

Harvesting an eligible hostile corpse preserves its loot and faction identity, leaves simplified remains, and grants randomized Soul Vigor: 2-4 Meager, 7-11 Worthy, 12-18 Potent, or 24-36 Prime. Light casting an owned servant instead unbinds it, returning Mana from its original investment and current Health plus its native soul and invested Vigor. The default Mana return is 50% at full Health; a newly summoned servant can return its investment but never creates a profit, and damage reduces the return.

Every summon also costs Soul Vigor. At Power 100, tier-one through tier-six spells cost 3/6/9/12/15/18. Power 0 doubles those costs and Power 200 halves them, rounded up, while corpse reanimation follows the same curve against that corpse's stable soul value.

Against a living hostile, Soul Rend deals a normal hero-attributed magical hit at 50%/100%/200% of a comparable tier-one light spell at Power 0/100/200. A surviving target gains one eight-second Claim stack, up to three; each adds 10% relative Claim chance and is consumed by the attempt. With Steel and Bone installed, the hit becomes Necrotic, making flesh, spirits, flora, and fungi vulnerable while corrupted, undead, skeletal, and constructed bodies increasingly resist it. Claim ignores those resistances, and living-target Soul Rend grants no Soul Vigor.

[b]Heavy cast - Bind or Claim[/b]

Heavy casting costs 30 base mana and challenges an eligible hostile corpse's binding resistance. Failed attempts save progress across reloads, while higher Power adds more progress per cast and Power 200 binds any eligible corpse in one. A valid hover previews [b]Reanimate: X Soul Vigor[/b] or [b]Requires X Soul Vigor[/b]. The cost is committed only after binding succeeds.

If the crosshair rests on nearby ground or harmless clutter, heavy Soul Rend can assist to an eligible corpse within 0.4 meters without selecting through walls or floors.

Living enemies at or below 40% Health can instead be [b]Claimed[/b]. Maximum chance rises from 5% at Power 0 to 17.5% at 100 and 30% at 200 before Health, quality, and Claim stacks, with a final cap of 35%. Meager, Worthy, Potent, and Prime enemies apply x1.00/x0.85/x0.65/x0.45 chance.

Failure leaves the enemy alive. Success awards the normal kill and XP once, then raises a reward-free servant through the protected reanimation lifecycle. Claim grants no immediate Soul Vigor, although the resulting servant can later be harvested for its corpse-quality reward.

| Meager | x0.75  | 2-4   | 4-8 / 2-4 / 1-2             | ~35 (+/-10%)  |
| Worthy | x0.90  | 7-11  | 14-22 / 7-11 / 4-6          | ~70 (+/-10%)  |
| Potent | x1.05  | 12-18 | 24-36 / 12-18 / 6-9         | ~115 (+/-10%) |

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Responsive Servants[/size][/color][/heading]

Soul and Service improves hero-owned summons only; ordinary NPC allies remain unchanged. Servants retain the game's native faction, ownership, combat logic, status effects, and damage types, while Summon Capacity builds on the native summon limit.

[b]Following[/b]

Idle servants trot when 4 meters behind, run at 8, and safely teleport at 60, with 1.25x catch-up speed outside combat. Guard, Hunt, Bulwark, Recall, and directed movement use stable, size-aware positions that keep servants separated, close empty ranks, accommodate large and Empowered bodies, and recover from blocked destinations without persistent spinning or crowding.

Guard, Close Guard, and Hunt ignore brief player movements, follow smoothly once movement becomes deliberate, and settle shortly after the hero stops. Bulwark Advance responds immediately while Sprint is held. Automatic catch-up teleports use the green-dark summoning effect, while native scene-transition relocation remains silent. Idle Movement Amount controls how often settled Guard and Hunt servants wander near their positions; zero disables voluntary wandering without disabling formation correction.

[b]Persistent Servants[/b] is enabled by default, keeping ordinary summons plus each raised servant's source identity, Health, Empowerment, investment, and Soulforged progress through saves, reloads, and game restarts. Generated raised copies never enter the native save graph. If persistence is disabled or one servant cannot be reconstructed safely, its protected source is restored from the same all-or-nothing recovery snapshot instead.

Ordinary summons remain on the game's native save path. Soul Rend-raised copies are instead rebuilt from one versioned host snapshot after the main scene is ready, keeping their Health, Empowerment, Soulforged progress, investment, and protected source relationship without placing the generated NPC tree in the native save graph. If a raised record cannot be trusted or reconstructed safely, its source is restored and trustworthy committed Soul Vigor is refunded.

[b]Awareness[/b]

Guard and Hunt consider faction hostility and line of sight from either the hero or acting servant, while Bulwark stays inside its short defensive zone. Steel and Bone contributes 80% of its added sight range and pursuit persistence within the game's native 45-meter summon leash. Explicit commands always take priority.

Optional passive crosshair sharing lets an uncommitted servant adopt the hostile under the hero's crosshair. It is disabled by default and remains governed by the selected behavior.

[b]Collision and native combat[/b]

[b]Summon Pass-Through[/b] lets the hero move through servants while they continue colliding with enemies, the world, and incoming attacks. The default [b]CombatOnly[/b] Player Attack Pass-Through mode also lets confirmed hero projectiles and magic-gauntlet contacts pass through servants during combat; Vanilla, MagicOnly, and AllProjectiles modes are available.

Replacement summons recover missing Invocation of Might scaling only when the outgoing summon proves it was active, leaving already-scaled stats untouched. Idle-loop volume defaults to 60%; combat sounds remain unchanged.

[heading][color=#38A840][size=5]Raised Servants, Upkeep, and Safety[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Safe reanimation[/b]

Heavy Soul Rend accepts ordinary hostile corpses and living enemies that can safely become native hero summons. Named or scripted characters, quest actors, merchants, guards, companions, bosses, minibosses, friendlies, claimed corpses, blocked targets, and enemies without safe summon data are rejected.

Successful binding protects the original corpse and creates an allied copy. When that servant dies, is dismissed, or is harvested, its original loot appears in simplified remains at its last safe position, with the corpse location as a fallback. Failed initialization safely restores the protected source instead. Persistent hosts reconnect the servant to that same protected corpse after loading rather than duplicating rewards.

Persistent reanimation additionally requires one canonical reusable NPC spawn template. Ambiguous or runtime-only spawn data is rejected before Soul Vigor is committed.

[b]Necromantic upkeep[/b]

At 0 Necromantic Power, one servant loses 2% maximum Health per minute, and each additional servant adds 1% upkeep to the entire host, capped at 8%. Upkeep continues during combat and can be lethal, but steadily falls with Power and disappears at Power 100.

[b]Rest and persistence[/b]

The default [b]Sustain[/b] rest behavior keeps the host but makes long rests costly. An eight-hour rest at Power 0 removes 45% maximum Health from one servant, with each additional servant adding 18% to the whole host up to a 90% cap. Shorter rests scale by their actual duration, Power steadily reduces the loss, and Power 100 removes it entirely. The rest screen previews the loss and warns when servants may perish. Choose [b]Dismiss[/b] instead to end the host through its normal safe remains lifecycle.

[b]Restore and Empower[/b]

Heavy Soul Rend restores one friendly servant by 20%/35%/50% maximum Health at Power 0/100/200. Below 95% Health, restoration is the cast's only service even when it fills the Health bar. At or above 95%, Soul Rend generously restores the remainder and, from Power 100 (1,000 Soul Vigor), can [b]Empower[/b] the servant in the same ritual.

Empower applies once per servant lifetime and costs twice the servant's stable base Soul Vigor value, reduced by the same Power curve as reanimation. The hover shows [b]Empower: X Soul Vigor[/b] or [b]Requires X Soul Vigor[/b], and payment occurs only when Empower succeeds. That investment can later be recovered in proportion to the servant's remaining Health.

A lower-biased 1.2x-1.5x combat roll improves both damage and resistance, while visible growth scales from 1.1x-1.3x. It cannot stack or be rerolled, but Empowered servants can still be restored.

[b]Necromantic presentation[/b]

Reanimation and Recall use the Forgotten Cemetery necromancer's green-dark summoning effect. Raised servants carry configurable bright-green arcs, a pale corpse-green core, and dark body-bound smoke; ordinary summons gain the treatment when they become Soulforged. Rank and Empowerment brighten the effect and draw all three colors toward the configurable [b]Full Potential Color[/b], white by default.

Every servant retains its native NPC portrait, with the vanilla skeleton-summon portrait as a fallback, so the summon HUD always has a valid image. Soul Rend hands emit a configurable no-shadow green light. Forty quality-matched ritual sounds give stronger souls greater weight, while separate light and heavy impact sounds make every valid Soul Rend contact feel immediate.

Version 3.1.0
Gave direct player arrows a distinct material identity layered onto their physical damage share instead of treating every bow hit as generic Pierce.
Made exposed humanoid flesh strongly vulnerable to arrows, ordinary flesh mildly vulnerable, and infected or sea flesh favorable without turning arrows into a universal answer.
Made armor, flesh undead, drowned corpses, flora or wood, spirits, constructs or stone, swarms, and confirmed skeletons progressively resistant to arrow impact.
Preserved Fire, Electric, and other non-physical arrow payload matchups by weighting them separately from the arrow's physical impact.
Added a mild preset-scaled weakness to direct player spell attacks against armored humanoids when vanilla has not already authored a reaction for the spell subtype.
Added independent ArrowMaterialRulesEnabled and ArmoredSpellWeaknessEnabled switches so either new system can be disabled without affecting the rest of Steel and Bone.
Reworked the Nexus description for narrower, reliable layouts with consistent section spacing and clearer material-combat emphasis.

Version 2.0.0
Expanded Steel and Bone into a lightweight but impactful difficulty mod while preserving its material weakness, resistance, and damage-number systems.
Added individually toggleable modifiers for incoming player damage, stamina usage, mana usage, enemy attack slots, enemy attack recovery, player-dealt poise damage, kill experience, quest and objective experience, and proficiency experience.
Made Tempered neutral for all new preset-controlled modifiers, Hardened apply a 5% penalty with one added attack slot, and Crucible apply a 10% penalty with two added attack slots.
Added an independent configurable multiplier for outgoing player health damage that is never changed by presets.
Added a six-slot default safety cap that limits only Steel and Bone's added enemy attack slots and never lowers a higher existing value.
Added conditional Custom Difficulty and Tainted Combat overlap detection with silent normal operation, one-time in-game warnings for active overlaps, and exact conflicting settings in the BepInEx log.
Kept coin gains and armor-weight penalties outside the 2.0.0 scope.
Reset the configuration schema because Preset now controls global damage, resource, combat-pressure, poise, and progression systems in addition to material-rule intensity.

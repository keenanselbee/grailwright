TargetVersion=4.0.0
BaselineVersion=3.9.6

Added EnemyMovementSpeedMultiplier as an x1.00 to x2.00 combat-movement tuning control. Presets retain their x1.00, x1.05, and x1.10 defaults, while players can raise the value afterward to match faster movement setups.
The existing safety tiers remain in place: ordinary agile enemies receive the full bonus, Medium and Elite enemies receive at most half, and Heavy, massive, boss, scripted, and non-pathing enemies retain native speed.
Split Blood and Necrotic magic into complementary biological and spiritual matchups across living flesh, undead, skeletons, constructs, spirits, sea creatures, flora, and fungus.
Blood damage now uses Blood Magic Expansion's exact damage provenance API when available, retaining text classification only as a compatibility fallback.

[center][color=#C94A4A][size=6]Steel and Bone[/size][/color]
[i]Thematic enemy resistances that make damage type matter more.[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Steel and Bone[/b] is a small BepInEx 5 Mono proof-of-concept resistance mod for [b]Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon[/b].

It gives a few enemy families stronger thematic defenses so damage type starts to matter more. Skeletons should not be easy prey for blood magic, stone should not care about poison, and Wyrd enemies should not fold cleanly to their own corruption.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Features[/size][/color][/heading]
[list]
[*]Three core presets: Bloodied, Forsaken, and Nightmare.[/*]
[*]Skeleton, bone, and animated-armor enemies resist blood magic and bleed.[/*]
[*]Skeleton, bone, and animated-armor enemies resist slashing and piercing, making blunt weapons the cleaner answer.[/*]
[*]Golem, stone, and construct enemies resist blood magic and poison.[/*]
[*]Wyrdspawn, Wyrd spirits, Wyrd slime, and Wyrdness enemies resist Wyrdness damage.[/*]
[*]All matching rules are checked, and the strongest resistance wins.[/*]
[*]Compact feedback text in the same default position as Killing Blow Mastery.[/*]
[*]Only one feedback word: Resistant.[/*]
[*]Resistant text shifts from orange-yellow toward deep red as the resistance gets stronger.[/*]
[*]Event-driven damage hook with cached target classification; no enemy scanning.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Default Balance[/size][/color][/heading]
[code=plaintext]
Rule                         | Bloodied | Forsaken | Nightmare
Harsh thematic counters      | 45%      | 25%      | 0%
Skeleton slashing/piercing   | 75%      | 55%      | 35%
[/code]

If more than one rule matches the same hit, the lowest damage multiplier is used. For example, a skeleton hit that is both bleed and slashing uses the harsher blood/bleed rule.

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Current Matchups[/size][/color][/heading]

[code=plaintext]
Enemy family                  | Resisted damage        | Notes
Skeleton / bone / armor       | Blood magic, bleed     | Harsh thematic counter
Skeleton / bone / armor       | Slashing, piercing     | Bludgeoning remains normal
Golem / stone / construct     | Blood magic, poison    | Harsh thematic counter
Wyrdspawn / Wyrd spirit       | Wyrdness               | Harsh thematic counter
[/code]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Configuration[/size][/color][/heading]
Start the game once to generate:

[code]
BepInEx/config/ks.tgfoa.steel-and-bone.cfg[/code]

Common entries:

[code]
Enabled = true
Preset = Forsaken
ResistanceTextEnabled = true
Diagnostics = false[/code]

[line]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Install[/size][/color][/heading]
Install with Vortex as a [b]BepInEx plugin[/b], or place the plugin folder here:

[code]
BepInEx/plugins/SteelAndBone/SteelAndBone.dll[/code]

[heading][color=#C94A4A][size=5]Notes[/size][/color][/heading]
This is an early combat-identity test rather than a full resistance overhaul. The goal is to prove the feel first, then expand into a broader Steel and Bone balance table only if it plays well.

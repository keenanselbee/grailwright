Version 0.1.7
Added configurable held-torch reach and world illumination with practical recommended defaults.
Added synchronized illumination-only flicker strength and speed controls that ride on the native torch update without changing flame particles or range.
Added independent live flame brightness and HDR bloom controls, both defaulting to the recommended 0.75.
Added a configurable soft radial flame corona for stronger bloom without solid geometry, another gameplay light, or a shadow caster.
Added a spatial small-fire loop anchored to the held torch or equipped hand, with displayed volume 1 as the recommended balance.
Added recommended Dungeon and Interior settings for Light Control plus First Person Arms Adjuster for first-person presentation.
Added automatic config backup, reset, and conservative settings recovery support.
Declared Torch Light Control incompatible to prevent both plugins from scaling the same held light.

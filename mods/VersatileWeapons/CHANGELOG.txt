Version 0.4.0
Added two enabled-by-default Reverse Hands compatibility settings that keep normal hand input while a paired spell is stowed by a two-handed grip and whenever exactly one spell is equipped.
Kept the game's Reverse Hands option as the master switch; two-spell loadouts can still use reversed input.

Version 0.3.9
Allowed a native one-handed weapon's post-loadout animation refresh to settle beside a spell or other non-melee hand, preventing the weapon model from remaining invisible after switching from a converted weapon and shield.

Version 0.3.8
Preserved the hero-wide drawn-weapon state when hiding a paired spell or other offhand item, so drawing after sheathing a two-handed grip no longer immediately returns the controls to the sheathed state.

Version 0.3.7
Stopped grip-FSM reconciliation while weapons are sheathed, preventing a two-handed grip from repeatedly reactivating its equip animation after a normal sheathe.

Version 0.3.6
Fixed equipment-menu weapon changes sometimes leaving both the one-handed and two-handed melee animation FSMs active, which could lock the first-person weapon model in its equip pose.
Extended native one-handed weapon refresh detection beyond loadout-index switches and added ongoing FSM reconciliation after the replacement animator becomes ready.

Version 0.3.5
Changed the default first-person Y alignment for native one-handed swords used with both hands from 0 to 0.02 meters.
Raised ConfigSchemaVersion from 10 to 11 because the sword-alignment default changed. Untouched old defaults receive the new alignment while compatible customized values remain eligible for recovery.

Version 0.3.4
Reorganized the generated config into six uniquely numbered sections with explicit native weapon and active grip directions.
Renamed the four base-potency settings from AtBaseStrength to AtWeaponRequirement so they cannot be confused with the character's unmodified Strength stat.
Renamed the diagnostics-only BasePotency simulation to WeaponRequirement for the same clearer endpoint terminology.
Raised ConfigSchemaVersion from 9 to 10 because every setting moved to a newly named or renumbered section and five settings were renamed. The previous config is backed up and clean current defaults are generated; retired section and setting names are intentionally skipped by previous-settings recovery.

Version 0.3.3
Removed Versatile Weapons' extra Strength lock so supported native two-handed weapons can occupy one hand as soon as the game's normal weapon requirement allows them.
Changed one-handed greatweapon scaling to begin at the weapon's base Strength requirement and reach full potency at FullPotencyStrengthMultiplier, which remains 2x by default.
Widened the default handling curve: base Strength now gives 75 percent damage, 50 percent attack speed, 60 percent poise, and 65 percent force; 2x Strength gives 100 percent damage, 75 percent attack speed, 95 percent poise, and 100 percent force.
Renamed the four minimum-Strength potency settings to BaseStrength and renamed the diagnostic MinimumPotency test endpoint to BasePotency for clearer config UX.
Raised ConfigSchemaVersion from 8 to 9 because the eligibility settings were removed, the base-potency settings and diagnostic enum value were renamed, and several potency defaults changed. Compatible same-name custom settings remain eligible for recovery while retired and renamed keys are skipped.
